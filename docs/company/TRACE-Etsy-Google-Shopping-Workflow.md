# TRACE — Etsy bestseller discovery and Google Shopping follow-up

Owner: Creative Director  
Date: 2026-10-06  
Status: Founder-discussed workflow documented; execution results must be recorded separately.

Governing standard: [TRACE v0.4.1](./TRACE-Bestseller-Creative-Intelligence-Methodology.md). This is an additional discovery route, not a replacement for all platform research. It does not alter the historical pilot.

## Goal

Find finished graphic T-shirts with useful commercial signals, investigate spread and variations, and decode their creative mechanisms. A verified Etsy Bestseller badge is sufficient commercial evidence to justify decoding an otherwise eligible design. Outside review counts, exact sales totals, original-creator identification and target-community transfer are not prerequisites.

## 1. Discover on Etsy

Start at the Founder-supplied URL:

https://www.etsy.com/search?q=t-shirts&instant_download=false&is_best_seller=true

Confirm the displayed Bestseller and Exclude digital downloads filters rather than relying on URL parameters alone. Record date, query, page and visible sort. The supplied screenshot shows Most relevant: page position is not a sales rank.

Inspect accessible pages, including page two and later pages. A later page does not negate a listing's Bestseller badge. Check the badge on the particular card or product page; do not silently equate Popular now, Star Seller, an ad, or mere inclusion in a filtered results page with an observed product-level Bestseller badge. If uncertain, record uncertainty and inspect the listing; uncertainty need not block exploratory decoding.

Record the product URL/listing ID, seller, visible design, badge wording and evidence location. Use screenshots or page observations accurately; distinguish a supplied screenshot from a fresh live check.

## 2. Exclude blanks, buyer-designed and bulk offers

This screening is the operator's responsibility, before selecting a source for decoding.

Exclude:
- blank garments;
- upload-your-own-design or custom-printing services;
- custom-text/name/message templates where the buyer supplies the core expression;
- wholesale, bulk-quantity and blank multipack offers;
- digital downloads and non-shirt services.

Inspect both the image and title/description. “Custom Shirt,” “Your Design,” “Front/Back Your Design,” wholesale/bulk language and buyer-upload instructions are useful signals. Open ambiguous listings to decide what is actually sold.

Keep finished designs selected for their existing creative idea. Ordinary size/color selection is not buyer-designed customization; ordinary retail quantity selection alone is not a bulk offer. Apply TRACE's separate franchise/brand exclusions.

Record a short exclusion reason. Continue to the next accessible candidate without routine Founder approval.

## 3. Search the distinctive expression

For a text-led design, search its distinctive wording on Google, then inspect the Shopping tab. Example:

“please be patient with me i'm from the 1900s” shirt

Use quoted and unquoted variants as needed; record the actual query. For a text-free shirt, describe the distinctive subject/action/relationship and compare visuals. This is a spread-and-variation search, not a mandatory origin investigation.

If Shopping is inaccessible, report that limitation and label ordinary web/product-page research as a fallback. Never claim to have inspected Shopping results when only general search was available.

## 4. Analyze non-sponsored Shopping results

Skip cards/blocks marked Sponsored or Ad and promotional cashback strips. The Founder wants these excluded from this comparison.

Record the observable query, date, locale and session context where available. Non-sponsored results are not guaranteed to be neutral, unpersonalized or exhaustive; their position is not proof of sales. Do not require a perfectly unbiased result set before learning.

Follow relevant non-sponsored listings to actual product pages. Identify merchants where visible: several cards from one marketplace do not establish several distinct sellers. Separate repeated product offers, marketplace listings, seller identities and design variations.

Inspect representative offers for:
- the actual phrase and artwork;
- the same versus materially different expression;
- visible product-specific reviews, purchase/order signals or bestseller indications;
- whether evidence is product-, design-family- or shop-wide;
- uncertainty, inaccessible widgets or conflicting information.

Gather accessible corroboration without making outside review verification a second admission gate. Missing sales numbers or reviews means that field is unknown, not that an Etsy-badged source is unsuitable for decoding.

## 5. Interpret spread and variations

| Observed pattern | Useful signal | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Same expression offered across distinct stores | Spread and seller adoption; supporting commercial evidence | Availability does not establish each seller's sales volume |
| Slightly different designs using the same joke | Adaptation and continued investment in the idea; supporting confidence | Cosmetic changes are not independent invention |
| Different original jokes/scenarios using the broader mechanism | Potential broader mechanism reuse | Explain the shared operation and assess independence |
| Product-specific purchase evidence on several offers | Additional demand support for the expression/family | Do not sum incompatible or pooled metrics |

Retain exact-copy and variation evidence; do not discard it simply because it is derivative. Group related offers so raw listing counts do not inflate independent mechanism corroboration. Record phrase-family relationships even when the artwork differs.

An Etsy Bestseller badge plus spread and variations strengthens the case for prioritizing the mechanism. The badge concerns a product; the decoded mechanism remains an interpretation, not proven sales causality. Unknown origin is not a filter.

## 6. Decode and report

Produce a concise source record and recipe:
- observed Etsy signal and its provenance;
- exclusion-screen result;
- Shopping query and accessible coverage, with ads excluded;
- representative source links, distinct merchants where identifiable, and variation notes;
- additional demand evidence and unresolved fields;
- abstract mechanism, source-grounding explanation and lightweight ingredients;
- confidence rationale and source-specific copying prohibitions.

Do not force a fixed number of sellers, reviews or variations before decoding. State a bounded scan scope and stop at useful coverage; incomplete access is recorded rather than turning the whole sample into a failure. Apply the current five-card quality checkpoint and documented replacement approach.

## Example discussed — “from the 1900s”

The supplied Etsy screenshot shows a Bestseller badge on the pictured finished shirt. The Google Shopping screenshot includes a Sponsored Products row dominated by Temu offers and a cashback strip; those blocks are excluded from the organic comparison.

General web research located phrase variants at Lucy Jaynes, Mongolife and Threadless. This did not establish an organic Google Shopping result count. Retrieved pages supported product availability and different visual treatments; design-specific sales/review strength was not fully confirmed.

Candidate interpretation: describe a familiar, relatively recent generational identity in language that makes it sound ancient, using exaggerated age as a humorous excuse. Wagon versus retro-computer treatments illustrate adaptations of the phrase, not independently proven mechanisms.

These observations illustrate the workflow, not a completed new bestseller scan or a causal finding. The Founder-provided badge is a source signal sufficient to justify decoding; further research enriches it rather than re-proving it.

## Operator checklist

1. Confirm Etsy filter state and individual badge evidence.
2. Exclude blank, buyer-designed, custom-service and bulk offers.
3. Search the phrase and inspect Shopping where accessible.
4. Exclude ads and cashback promotions.
5. Open representative products; record spread, variations and accessible purchase evidence.
6. Decode the grounded mechanism without origin or target-fit gates.
7. Report coverage, evidence strength and uncertainty accurately.
