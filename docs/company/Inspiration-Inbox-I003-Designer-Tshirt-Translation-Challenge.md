# Inspiration Inbox I003 — Identity Truth to Wearable T-Shirt

**Status:** Inbox

**Category:** Creative / Product Design

**Source:** Founder feedback during R012 concept development on 2026-08-13.

## Observation

Many of our strongest concepts are deeply authentic to the identity community. Keep those coming.

The recurring challenge is turning an indirect insider truth, ritual, emotion, or joke into a T-shirt people genuinely want to wear. The final result must feel like strong apparel, not an explanation, infographic, poster, or research artifact.

## Question to Revisit

How do we preserve deep identity recognition while making the final object visually desirable, wearable, and culturally fluent as a designer T-shirt?

This is intentionally parked as an open problem. We should learn through continued design work, apparel references, comparison, and accumulated examples rather than forcing a premature rule.
