# TRACE Bestseller Creative Intelligence Methodology

## Purpose and scope

TRACE builds a reusable library of creative concepts and mechanisms decoded from commercially promising T-shirts. Its primary output is an explanation of the creative play: the relationship, transformation, recognition, contrast, or reveal that plausibly gives an idea its appeal, plus the ingredients needed to express that mechanism.

Graphic composition and apparel execution are supplementary learning. Grammar-only observations do not substitute for the core mechanism-library goal.

TRACE produces interpretations, not proof of what caused external sales. Record what was observed separately from what the analyst infers.

**TRACE:** source evidence → source decode → abstract mechanism and ingredients → recipe library.

**Downstream concept development:** recipe + target-community insight → original concept → design → bounded market testing.

Target-community research, applicability to current wedges, concept transfer tests, advertising and sales validation are outside TRACE acceptance requirements. They can later inform the library without blocking its initial entries. This update changes TRACE only; it does not rewrite other company playbooks or authorize expenditure.

## 1. Source selection and evidence

Start with broad cross-niche sources with commercial signals; do not select only what the analyst finds clever. Record why each source is worth studying and distinguish exploratory inspiration from commercially supported examples.

Use the strongest available product-specific evidence and preserve its scope:

- Attributable purchase/order signals and product-specific reviews support demand more directly than mere availability.
- Verified platform bestseller status or sales-based collection order provides contextual evidence; record time window, attribution and limitations.
- Seller bestseller statements and widespread recurrence are supporting signals, with weaker confidence when uncorroborated.
- Search position, ads, likes and shop-wide totals must not be reported as design-level sales.

There is no universal 100-review, 180-day-recency, nonzero-sales-documentation or native-sort threshold for entry into mechanism analysis. Record counts, dates and sort verification when available; their absence reduces the strength of a commercial claim rather than automatically blocking exploratory decoding. Known zero sales cannot support a claim of commercial success.

Label the commercial evidence strength with a brief rationale. Weak or unavailable demand evidence permits exploratory analysis but does not establish a commercially supported winner. Do not combine unlike platform metrics into a fabricated numerical score.

Preserve the v0.3 evidence dimensions: demand signal, design attribution, currency and persistence. Record source URL, seller, observation date, visible wording/imagery, review scope, uncertainty, access mode and expression-family ID as available. Mark missing fields explicitly.

### Sampling procedures versus admission rules

The platform traversal, sampling-frame capture, quotas, source caps, access stop rules and no-backfill procedures in archived v0.3 §3.1–3.4 were not reviewed or changed in this discussion. Retain them when executing that structured scan protocol; a new experiment must state its protocol prospectively. Do not change a frozen scan after seeing its results.

A missing complete frame or unverified sort prevents a claim to have completed that ranked sample. It does not prevent a separately labeled exploratory decode of an accessible source. Such a decode cannot be inserted into a frozen sample or used to fill its quota.

Former origin-eligibility and commercial-evidence thresholds embedded in those platform procedures are superseded by the current rules here. For example, Etsy review counts now describe evidence rather than impose a universal decode cutoff, and a store with unverified sorting may supply an exploratory example without supplying a verified sales rank.

## 2. Source eligibility and origin

### Familiarity and franchise dependence

An ordinary animal, object, occupation, cultural practice or historical subject is not disqualified because audience recognition contributes to the idea. Historical-reference humor may be decoded, with the role of recognition recorded.

A generic penguin is eligible on this distinction. A specific penguin character from Happy Feet remains excluded under the founder's franchise rule. The Jefferson deadline example may be studied as a mechanism without copying its illustration or caption.

Retain exclusions for specific entertainment franchises and characters, sports/organization insignia, celebrity merchandise, corporate marks and established seller-logo merchandise under the existing scope. Historical reference alone is no longer treated as celebrity/franchise dependence. Do not automatically reject a subject merely because its appeal would change if all recognizable references disappeared.

Actual franchise or brand dependence is a separate question from unknown authorship. Investigate concrete ambiguity about that dependence when it affects eligibility; do not use general uncertainty about who created an image as a substitute exclusion.

### Origin is not a filter

Unknown creator, unclear first publication, cross-seller recurrence or unresolved illustration/phrase provenance must not, by themselves, prevent mechanism analysis, a Source Decode Card or a candidate recipe.

There is no mandatory origin-search checklist before every decode. Record the observed source and uncertainty. Investigate origin when it resolves a concrete franchise-dependency or independence question. Creator attribution is useful when available but is not a prerequisite.

Do not reintroduce the old origin gate through a required confidence field, unresolved-status label, maturity rule or demand for proof of authorship. Historical IP-QUARANTINE labels remain in old records; for new analysis, use a nonblocking origin note unless a separate actual exclusion applies.

Source-study permission is not permission to reproduce its wording or artwork. Keep source-specific copying prohibitions with the handoff.

### Recurrence and independence

Group the same artwork or commercial expression across sellers, garment variants and platforms as one expression family. Record links and visible differences.

Widespread repetition is a supporting commercial signal: sellers consider an expression worth offering, potentially because they observed demand. Availability alone does not establish sales volume. Product-specific purchase/review signals strengthen the inference.

Copies can support observations about that expression's spread or demand; they do not become independent examples of the abstract mechanism. Different original expressions using the same mechanism may support broader corroboration. Record uncertainty about independence without blocking the initial candidate decode.

## 3. Source Decode Card

Keep observed expression, source context, commercial evidence and analytical interpretation separate.

Record:
- source and expression-family identity, links and evidence limitations;
- the wording/imagery necessary to understand the idea;
- source-audience context and insider truth when relevant;
- setup, transformation, tension/reveal and proposed payoff;
- candidate explanation, alternative explanations and confidence;
- source-specific elements that must not be copied;
- optional composition observations.

Source context helps explain the original idea. It does not impose a requirement to research a future target community.

Retain the primary outcomes RECIPE-CANDIDATE, GRAMMAR-ONLY and NO-TRANSFERABLE-MECHANISM. Refine an initially vague mechanism before concluding none can be articulated; do not force a mechanism or treat composition output as fulfillment of the main goal.

The five-card review checkpoint in archived v0.3 §4.2 remains in place for structured calibration work. Apply the revised eligibility rules here: unknown origin is a recordable uncertainty, not a failed checkpoint.

## 4. Recipe structure and acceptance

A recipe describes a reusable transformation, not a source-specific shirt template.

> Given a familiar frame and an input with an abstract property or behavior, apply an operation so the audience resolves a tension into an emotional or social payoff.

### Specificity

Keep the requirement to be more informative than “use humor,” “make it relatable” or “make it vintage.” Explain enough to guide creative choices and distinguish fitting from nonfitting concepts. Treat weak specificity first as a refinement task.

### Source example and abstraction

The abstract mechanism should not depend on the source's exact wording, specific character, artwork or distinctive composition. Keep the source example alongside it for explanation and traceability.

Remove the source-erasure test: a reader recognizing or remembering the source does not invalidate a recipe. Assess actual copying and superficial substitutions in downstream concepts, rather than banning useful explanation at the recipe stage.

### Ingredients

Distinguish required, adjustable, optional and prohibited ingredients in a lightweight form. Initial ingredient assignments are interpretive hypotheses; refine them with versioned reasoning. Avoid unnecessary taxonomy.

### No target-community acceptance test

No target-community evidence, frozen transfer panel, three unrelated truth instantiations or successful application to current wedges is required to extract, complete or retain a TRACE recipe.

Describe abstract applicability conditions when useful. Do not require proof that the current three communities supply them. A mechanism can remain useful for future audiences.

### Explanatory-power standard — discussion pending

No change is approved yet to the existing requirement that a candidate explanation be grounded in the source. Retain the existing support-audit requirement below pending discussion; do not report the founder as having approved its retention, removal or modification during this session.

Before assigning or raising maturity, maintain a support-audit matrix with one row per credited SDC and one column per required recipe ingredient. Every credited source must demonstrate every required ingredient in its observed record. An ingredient introduced, tightened, or clarified during transfer testing is a new candidate refinement; it cannot retroactively increase external maturity. Reclassify unsupported ingredients or remove the source, then recalculate maturity.

## 5. Evidence labels and library use

| Label | Evidence description |
|---|---|
| CANDIDATE | Mechanism extracted from one eligible expression family and its decode; origin identification is not required |
| PROVISIONALLY-CORROBORATED | At least two non-derivative mechanism analogues from unrelated niches and independent sellers |
| CORROBORATED | At least three non-derivative expression families across at least two unrelated niches and two independent sellers |
| INTERNALLY-TESTED | A downstream original instantiation received a predeclared behavioral test |
| REPLICATED | At least two materially different original concepts or niche inputs show the relevant behavior |
| RETIRED | Evidence or relevance warrants retirement; preserve the reason and history |

These are evidence descriptors, not minimum qualifications for using a candidate in concept development. Commercial evidence strength remains separate from the number of mechanism examples. Corroboration does not establish the cause of sales.

Origin uncertainty alone cannot bar corroboration. Count visible independent applications with a stated rationale; do not count unresolved copies as independent or require identifying the first creator of every example.

Do not use “validated” without saying what was assessed. Library acceptance, external recurrence and actual customer demand are different judgments.

## 6. Downstream boundary

The Creative Director concept/design playbooks and MVV methodology govern application to target communities, original execution and market tests. Their requirements were not changed by this TRACE-only revision.

Carry forward recipe ID/version, source examples and source-specific copying prohibitions. Check actual concepts for close copying, distinctive phrase scaffolds and superficial substitutions; assess artwork provenance before production. No TRACE study grants permission to reuse source assets.

Downstream results may update recipe confidence. A failed shirt tests a combination of audience, mechanism, wording, artwork, offer and delivery; it does not by itself refute the mechanism. Clicks indicate attention; purchasing behavior supplies stronger demand evidence.

TRACE can finish its library entry before any downstream concept or test exists.
