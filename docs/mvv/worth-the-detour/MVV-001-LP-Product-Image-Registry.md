# MVV-001 — Landing-Page Product Image Registry

## Purpose

Record the approved customer-facing primary landing-page image for each product in the MVV #1 assortment without replacing or modifying the canonical locked T-shirt design source.

The governing presentation standard is:

`docs/mvv/worth-the-detour/MVV-001-LP-Product-Image-Standard.md`

The canonical assortment is:

`docs/mvv/worth-the-detour/MVV-001-LP-Assortment-Manifest.md`

## Important distinction

LP primary images are **presentation derivatives** for customer evaluation. They are not new T-shirt designs and must never replace the locked creative source assets.

The locked source remains authoritative for artwork, print composition, color relationships, and intended product identity. The LP image may alter merchandising presentation only within the approved product-image standard.

### Approved presentation treatment

B2 establishes the initial calibration reference for the MVV #1 product-image system:

- mobile-first portrait composition;
- front-facing, highly inspectable garment presentation;
- intended Comfort Colors 1717 ivory / natural garment character retained;
- relaxed, substantial cotton-jersey appearance rather than decorative or embossed fabric texture;
- restrained rolled-sleeve styling is acceptable;
- warm antique-shop environment may establish context, but background remains subordinate and softly defocused;
- shirt remains the unmistakable visual hero;
- merchandising props remain peripheral and do not obscure or compete with the design;
- locked artwork wording, composition, colors, relative scale, and placement remain authoritative;
- image is optimized for fair product evaluation on mobile, not Meta-feed instant readability.

### Calibration principle for remaining products

Use B2 as a **quality and hierarchy calibration**, not as a rigid template. Remaining products may use different styling when appropriate to their gender/style expression or design character, but should remain broadly comparable in garment scale, presentation polish, inspectability, and visual opportunity.

Do not force every product into the same hanger, antique props, or exact background. Coherence should come from product truth, image hierarchy, mobile-first framing, restrained merchandising, and comparable presentation quality.

### Archive verification

B2 is therefore the first fully archived standardized primary LP product image for MVV #1.
