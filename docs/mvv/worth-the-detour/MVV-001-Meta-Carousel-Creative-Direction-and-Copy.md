# MVV-001 Meta Carousel Creative Direction and Copy

## Final concept decision

**Concept:** _Ten ways to know the hunt._

Advance one manually assembled, image-only carousel containing all 10 executable MVV #1 products. It is one coherent acquisition execution and receives one persistent carousel-level identifier:

- `creative_id`: `WTD-MVV001-CAR-01`
- objective: Traffic
- destination: Website
- performance goal: Maximize number of link clicks
- placements: Facebook Feed and Instagram Feed only
- ordering: enable Meta's predicted-best-performing-card ordering if the live setup supports it

The carousel should feel like a curated rack discovered inside the antiques/vintage-hunting world. Each card presents an unmistakable garment and one distinct recognition signal: detour behavior, a trained eye, find logistics, booth culture, discount ritual, direct treasure-hunter identity, or the thrill of discovery.

The carousel is not a product-winner ranking. Preserve:

`WTD-MVV001-CAR-01 → source card + source-product design_id → landing visit → independent downstream design_id behavior → lead`

A visitor may click one shirt and prefer another. High card CTR does not prove product preference; under-delivery does not reject a product.

## Shared visual system

Use the approved LP customer-facing product images as the presentation source and the locked design assets as the authority for artwork. Adapt by cropping and resizing only; do not regenerate, reinterpret, add to, or enlarge the locked print independently of the garment.

Every card must:

- show apparel unmistakably, including enough collar, sleeve, or garment body to avoid a poster/artwork read;
- keep the complete print unit within the safe crop;
- retain restrained, softly defocused antique/vintage-discovery context;
- use comparable garment scale and warm-ivory color treatment;
- contain no added on-image explanation or campaign badge in the first preview;
- work without positional dependence because Meta may reorder it;
- receive compositionally equivalent 1080 × 1080 and 1080 × 1350 adaptations.

Interface copy does the extra decoding where a print is intentionally quiet. Essential meaning must not depend on a preceding card, a tiny support line, or an optional description.

## Final Meta copy package

### Shared primary text — `COPY-P01`

> The best finds rarely announce themselves. These are T-shirts for antique-mall detours, flea-market hunts, estate-sale mornings—and people who always check one more booth.

This is the sole first-run primary-text version. It establishes apparel and category, recognizes behavior, and avoids explaining the entire brand.

Descriptions may not render in every placement. No essential category meaning depends on this field.

### CTA

**Learn More.** It accurately describes the Traffic campaign and landing-page visit without implying immediate purchase availability.

### Copy guardrails

Do not add trivial variants before the preview gate. Do not use thrift-haul, reseller, appraisal, farmhouse, generic nostalgia, or travel language. Live preview must confirm headline and primary-text truncation before bulk export.

## Complementary non-carousel decision

**Do not produce the behavioral-scene concept for the first run.**

A “five-minute antique stop” lifestyle scene is meaningfully different from the product-led carousel, but the expected MVV budget is too small to support a second acquisition mechanism without materially diluting delivery and interpretation. The carousel already contains 10 product signals and is the direct test of Meta's matching role in the business thesis.

Retain the behavioral scene as the first reserve concept. Produce it only after one of these conditions is documented:

- the carousel cannot acquire relevant clicks;
- a later budget explicitly supports a format-level comparison;
- the Campaign Lead identifies a learning question the carousel cannot answer.

If activated later, it must receive a different `creative_id`; it cannot reuse `WTD-MVV001-CAR-01`.

## Representative preview gate

Selected card: **`C01` / `Type-Led-4`**.

Why: it was designed for strong phone-thumbnail impact and stress-tests both expressive script and the small antiques-decoding line in the two approved ratios. Its role as the representative production check is independent of its supplied position.

Preview files:

- `WTD-MVV001-CAR-01_C01_Type-Led-4_FB-Feed_1x1_1080.jpg`
- `WTD-MVV001-CAR-01_C01_Type-Led-4_IG-Feed_4x5_1080x1350.jpg`

These are preview candidates, not final deployment approval. Before bulk export, the Campaign Operator must verify:

- placement-specific media assignment works for one card inside the manual carousel;
- Meta does not substitute an uncontrolled crop;
- garment, script, support line, and antiques context render acceptably on realistic mobile previews;
- `C01` retains its own destination/source identity;
- best-performing-card-first does not detach card media from its URL;
- primary text, headline, description, and Learn More CTA render as expected.

If any item fails, stop and make the smallest documented campaign-structure or crop correction before producing the other 18 files.

## Remaining approvals and role gates

- **Campaign Operator:** live Ads Manager preview, placement-media assignment, automatic ordering availability, and card-to-URL integrity.
- **Technical Reviewer:** exact URL/parameter convention and confirmation that source-card/source-product identity remains separate from downstream `design_id`.
- **Strategy Advisor:** approve this final copy package and the recommendation not to add the reserve concept.
- **Founder:** approve the final creative package and spending before launch.
- **Creative Director:** bulk-export remaining cards only after the operator gate, then review actual placement previews and issue the final creative-readiness decision.

The earlier 1:1-versus-4:5 document tension is fully resolved by the merged production requirements: both ratios are required because Facebook Feed and Instagram Feed are the approved first-run placements. Stories, Reels, 9:16, video, and all other ratios remain excluded.
