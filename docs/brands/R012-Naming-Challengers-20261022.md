## Recommendation

Rank the new challengers **A Fine Excuse → Rummalark → Rummage Inclined**. These are candidates for discussion, not a replacement decision. Still a Find remains the Founder-approved current champion.

“Green” means no substantial **name-specific** apparent conflict was identified in the bounded exact/selected-variant federal and commercial searches below, and the Brand Manager considers the complete name worth discussing. It does not mean the words are unused, registration is assured, rights are available everywhere, or domains/handles are available. Shared ordinary words are assessed in the complete name rather than automatically disqualifying every combination. Rummage Inclined has the greatest surrounding commercial crowding of these three.

## Ranked candidates

### 1. A Fine Excuse — preliminary green to advance to comparison

**A Fine Excuse**  
*For people who find stuff. And reasons to bring it home.*

- Four syllables, familiar spelling, natural spoken rhythm.
- “Fine” suggests both a good justification and the quality/character of a find. The whole name knowingly admits that the desired object came before the explanation.
- The line identifies the finder community and explains the joke. The pair feels friendly and self-aware without mentioning a spouse or assuming either gender.
- Extends to records, restoration supplies, sea glass and other objects worth pursuing.
- Weakness: little history/vintage meaning without the line; “excuse” may imply an apology or dubious justification. Avoid implying poor product quality, excuses for service failures, or irresponsible spending.
- Exact/selected federal variants: no results in the submitted combined query.
- Broader fine + excuse/selected sound-spelling combinations in apparel/retail: no name-specific result in the inspected result sets.
- Commercial searches: ordinary phrase use, including [Moss sale-shopping editorial](https://www.moss.co.uk/inside-pocket/post/how-to-shop-the-sale), rather than an identified exact-name apparel business. An indexed [Fine Excuse Pinterest profile](https://de.pinterest.com/fineexcuse/) exists; its page could not be fetched (403), so activity/ownership are unknown and the handle is not presumed available.
- Signed-in [Facebook Pages search](https://www.facebook.com/search/pages/?q=A%20Fine%20Excuse) displayed unrelated excuse-word pages; no exact/strongly similar full-name business in displayed results. Not exhaustive.
- Existing EXCUSE-related apparel marks/commerce exist. The broad 282-result query was not exhaustively reviewed. Examples actually inspected include live MAKE MOVES NOT EXCUSES (88820577), live NO EXCUSES JUST RESULTS (78177195), and dead/abandoned EXCUSE (88883686). Their different complete wording is why they did not become automatic blockers for this full phrase; dead status is not proof of absent commercial rights.

### 2. Rummalark — preliminary green to advance to comparison

**Rummalark**  
*For people who find stuff. And reasons to bring it home.*

- Coined from the sound/idea of rummaging and “a lark”: the hunt is an enjoyable little adventure.
- Intended reading **RUM-uh-lark**; three syllables, playful and more distinctive in form than a generic Finds boutique name.
- The line gives immediate meaning even before the coined construction is explained.
- Weakness: a first-time listener may need spelling confirmation, especially single/double m. “Lark” as a playful outing is less immediately familiar to some U.S. customers. Rummage/history associations are evocative rather than explicit.
- Exact and selected spelling/phonetic variants returned no result in the combined all-class federal query.
- Broader RUMMAL/RUMMEL/RUMAL/RUMEL apparel/retail search inspected both result pages. ER <ELIAS RUMELIS> (87392248) is a live registered apparel mark; EIL/ELI BY ELIAS RUMELIS (90522084/90522098) appeared dead/abandoned. The full names, syllable structure and commercial impression differ materially at this early screen. This is a screening judgment, not an adjudication.
- Exact/variant web searches identified no name-specific commercial business in returned results. Some exact-token hits were fragments of romanized Tamil religious texts, not brands.
- Signed-in [Facebook Pages search](https://www.facebook.com/search/pages/?q=Rummalark) showed “We didn't find any results.”
- Surrounding Rummage commerce is documented below; the coined complete word is not represented as a right to the generic Rummage name.

### 3. Rummage Inclined — preliminary green to advance to comparison; most crowded surrounding field

**Rummage Inclined**  
*For people who find stuff. And reasons to bring it home.*

- Four syllables. Names a disposition: someone naturally inclined to browse, dig and discover.
- Strongest immediate antique/thrift-hunter cue of this trio. The name identifies the habit; the line adds the rationalization.
- More about the finder than an object already in their possession.
- Weakness: less emotionally warm and less distinctive than the first two; “rummage” can suggest a resale shop or low-price jumble. Expansion to sea glass/restoration is possible but less direct than records/antiques.
- No exact/selected phrase variants in the submitted federal query; no full-name business identified in the returned commercial results or displayed signed-in Facebook Pages results.
- [Facebook search](https://www.facebook.com/search/pages/?q=Rummage%20Inclined) returned numerous **Rummage** pages, including clothing and retail categories. Their existence is positive evidence of a crowded shared-word field, not evidence that the full proposed name is in use.
- Important neighboring records: RUMMAGE (77282260), dead/cancelled, formerly apparel; ROCKS AND RUMMAGE (90811515), live/registered online retail featuring crystals/jewelry; RUMMAGER (78663288), dead/abandoned, including branded apparel. INCLYNE (97625815), pending apparel, and THE INCLINE CLUB (90647402), registered apparel, also appeared in the broader second-word screen.
- Important commercial neighbors: [Rummage community thrift, Vancouver](https://www.rummagecommunitythrift.com/about), [Rummage Stretch vintage athletic wear](https://www.rummagestretch.com/shop), [Rummage Studio bags/accessories](https://www.rummagestudio.com/collections), and indexed Rummage Style apparel use. Rummage Style's fetched homepage now says it is being worked on; do not infer closure.
- Basis for preliminary pass: no close full-phrase/sound neighbor identified; ordinary “rummage” describes the audience's activity and the complete phrase makes a different impression from the named businesses. This is a narrower green than “commercially clean.” If the Founder wants the least crowded name form, favor Rummalark over this candidate. Do not shorten this candidate to Rummage.

## Commercial method and limits

Two search engines were used for exact names, unspaced names, selected spelling/wording variants, and related apparel/retail uses. Follow-ups included Instagram/Facebook/Etsy/TikTok indexed searches. TikTok retrieval was robots-excluded; these were not direct in-app handle checks. Some engines relaxed quoted constraints or returned irrelevant/ordinary-language results. Those are weak negative evidence, not proof of absence.

Signed-in Facebook Pages searches were directly observed for all three finalists. No location restriction was applied. Results were personalized/ranked and only displayed results were inspected. An exact profile/handle sweep and domain availability check were not completed; no asset availability claims are made.

No exhaustive state-register search, full common-law/phonetic database review, international clearance, ownership/territorial-priority analysis or attorney assessment was performed. The [USPTO recommends a comprehensive search beyond exact federal matches](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks). All conclusions are preliminary screening/business judgments as of this research session, not a guarantee of legal availability.

## Founder review

Compare each full pair against Still a Find. The decision is whether any new name feels substantially better, not whether an empty search compels selection. A Fine Excuse is the strongest new creative challenger; Rummalark offers the most distinctive coined form; Rummage Inclined is the most directly niche-readable but has the weakest commercial distinctiveness.

No new candidate is adopted. No purchases, accounts, filings, paid engagement, live copy changes or garment changes were made.
