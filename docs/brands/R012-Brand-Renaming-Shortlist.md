# R012 Brand Renaming Shortlist

## Purpose

Preserve the current naming decision and strongest alternatives while the company evaluates a replacement for **Worth the Detour** before store launch.

This document is a working naming record, not a final legal clearance or launch approval.

## Current Lead Candidate

### 1. Drift & Keep — LEAD CANDIDATE

**Current decision:** Top candidate to replace **Worth the Detour**.

Why it is strong:

- combines serendipitous discovery with the act of choosing what is worth keeping;
- feels natural for the initial antiques / vintage treasure-hunter wedge;
- can plausibly stretch to adjacent identity communities such as old-house restoration, sea-glass or shell collecting, records, inherited objects, and other hobbies organized around discovery, preservation, and story;
- has enough movement and personality to work as an apparel brand rather than sounding like a museum, preservation nonprofit, or literary project;
- carries some of the journey energy that made **Worth the Detour** attractive without copying that name's construction.

Current availability signal:

- preliminary exact-name web / commercial-use screening did not surface an obvious exact-name apparel or business collision;
- preliminary trademark screening did not surface an obvious exact-match blocker;
- **not legally cleared**. Deeper commercial, trademark, domain, and social-handle diligence is still required before launch adoption.

## Fallback / Reference Shortlist

These names preserve the strongest language territories explored during the September 2026 naming exercise. They are listed so the team can recover the work quickly if **Drift & Keep** fails diligence.

### 2. Kept & Carried

**Territory:** preservation + story + personal attachment.

Why it remains strong:

- emotionally captures objects being preserved and carried forward;
- fits antiques, heirlooms, old houses, records, sea glass, and similar communities;
- feels tactile and wearable enough for a T-shirt brand.

Availability note:

- **Caution.** Exact phrase is already used in art / retail contexts, including an exhibition whose subject matter overlaps closely with memory, inherited objects, preservation, and story.
- No final legal clearance has been performed.

### 3. Quiet Worth

**Territory:** discernment + overlooked value.

Why it remains useful:

- strongly expresses the hunter-eye worldview: valuable things do not always announce themselves;
- maps closely to **For people who see what others miss**;
- compact, memorable, and apparel-friendly.

Availability note:

- **Potential blocker.** Existing apparel / clothing use under **QuietWorth / Quiet Worth** was identified during preliminary screening.
- Retain as naming inspiration, not as a currently preferred fallback without new diligence.

### 4. Wayfound

**Territory:** journey + discovery.

Why it remains useful:

- modern, compact, and highly brandable in form;
- preserves some of the discovery / journey energy of **Worth the Detour**;
- strong visual and apparel-label potential.

Availability note:

- **Potential blocker.** Existing commercial uses and U.S. apparel-related trademark activity were identified during preliminary screening.
- Retain as naming inspiration, not as a currently preferred fallback without new diligence.

### 5. Lorebound

**Territory:** story + carrying forward + journey.

Why it remains useful:

- conceptually rich across antiques, old houses, records, books, collections, and objects with accumulated history;
- carries both story and movement in one compact word.

Availability note:

- **Potential blocker.** Multiple existing commercial uses were identified during preliminary screening.
- Also carries some literary / fantasy semantic gravity that may be less ideal for a broad T-shirt brand.

### 6. Storyborne

**Territory:** story + continuity + carrying forward.

Why it remains useful:

- semantically strong for objects, places, and people that carry stories through time;
- naturally accommodates both the story of an object and the story of how it was found.

Availability note:

- **Potential blocker.** An active U.S. business already uses Storyborne in a closely related story / memory / apparel context.
- Do not advance without materially different diligence evidence.

### 7. Storybound

**Territory:** story + attachment / connection.

Why it remains useful:

- intuitive and memorable;
- captures attachment to objects and stories.

Availability note:

- **Potential blocker.** Existing Storybound businesses and apparel / book-oriented uses were identified.
- Linguistically it also skews more strongly toward books, publishing, and reading than the broader R012 worldview.

## Naming Territory Learned So Far

The strongest names tend to sit at the intersection of:

1. **Discovery / serendipity** — the pleasure of wandering, detouring, and finding something unexpected.
2. **Discernment / overlooked value** — seeing worth, character, or possibility that others miss.
3. **Keeping / preservation** — choosing what deserves to remain, be restored, or be carried forward.
4. **Story / lived history** — both the prior life of the object and the story of how the finder encountered it.

A useful synthesis is:

> **Notice something others might pass by, discover why it matters, and become the next person in its story.**

The replacement name should feel at home with antique / vintage treasure hunters immediately enough to support the R012 wedge, while remaining capable of expanding to adjacent communities that share the same worldview without forcing the fit.

## Current Ranking / Action

1. **Drift & Keep** — advance to deeper naming diligence.
2. **Kept & Carried** — strongest true fallback, but carries a caution flag.
3. **Quiet Worth** — strong brand language; current commercial-use concern.
4. **Wayfound** — strong apparel form; current commercial / trademark concern.
5. **Lorebound** — strong concept; crowded and somewhat literary.
6. **Storyborne** — strong semantics; close commercial collision.
7. **Storybound** — useful reference; crowded and book-coded.

If **Drift & Keep** fails deeper diligence, do **not** automatically adopt a red-flagged name above. Reassess **Kept & Carried** first, then use the strongest language territories in this document to generate a fresh round of candidates before settling for a known-crowded option.

- No public-facing rename should occur until deeper diligence and Founder approval are complete.
- **Worth the Detour** remains the working MVV brand until an explicit rename decision is made.
