# R012 Concept C3B — The Thrill Curve

**Creative Territory:** C — The Find Has a Story / behavioral hunt emotion

**Core recognition:** treasure hunters know the emotional rise of finding better discount stickers and the abrupt disappointment of discovering that the item is already sold.

## Founder-approved visual decisions

- Unisex first-MVV execution on the controlled warm off-white / ivory garment.
- Center-chest horizontal composition.
- Three bright, real-world-inspired discount dots: `10% OFF`, `25% OFF`, `50% OFF`.
- Dot colors are vivid estate-sale colors: neon/bright green, bright yellow, vivid orange.
- Discount dots progress left-to-right from mild interest to increasing delight.
- A compact antique-style red string `SOLD` tag is the final object in the sequence.
- The SOLD tag is sized to remain clearly visible but must not dominate or look obviously larger than the discount stickers.
- All emotion faces sit on one consistent horizontal baseline beneath their corresponding objects.
- Emotional progression: neutral/mild at 10%, pleased at 25%, strongly happy at 50%, abrupt disappointment under SOLD.
- The concept should read immediately as a simple emotional sequence, not as an infographic.
- Keep the graphic clean, playful, authentic, and wearable with generous surrounding negative space.
- Front print only for the controlled first-MVV execution.
