# R012 Concept C3B — The Thrill Curve

**Creative Territory:** C — The Find Has a Story / behavioral hunt emotion

**Core recognition:** treasure hunters recognize the emotional rise of finding better discount stickers and the abrupt disappointment of discovering that an item is already sold.

## Locked visual decisions

- Unisex first-MVV execution on the controlled warm off-white / ivory garment.
- Center-chest horizontal composition.
- Three bright discount dots: `10% OFF`, `25% OFF`, `50% OFF`.
- Dot colors: bright green, bright yellow, vivid orange.
- A compact antique-style red `SOLD` string tag ends the sequence.
- The SOLD tag stays subordinate enough to preserve balance.
- All four emotion faces sit on one horizontal baseline.
- Emotional progression: neutral/mild at 10%, pleased at 25%, strongly happy at 50%, disappointed under SOLD.
- Keep the graphic clean, playful, authentic, wearable, and surrounded by generous negative space.
- Front print only for the controlled first-MVV execution.
