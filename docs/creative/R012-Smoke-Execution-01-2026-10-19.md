## R-A v2 — Founder-directed shortening

> I DIDN'T NEED IT.  
> BEFORE SOMEONE ELSE BOUGHT IT.

Essential visual for a later render: an empty-handed shopper watches another shopper carry away a distinctive used lamp bearing a SOLD tag; a small flea-market sign establishes the setting. Do not add explanatory captions or thought bubbles.

E002/E011 support missed-find regret. Wording follows the Founder's suggested revision. It creatively exaggerates unavailability as the cause of desire; in E011, desire preceded discovering the object gone. Free-development route, no forced recipe attribution.

Provisional evaluation: shorter than the original, with another buyer now explicitly named. The fragment after the full stop is intentional but may interrupt reading. The visual must establish the secondhand-find context. Humor is not assumed improved until reviewed. Original 3/5 and the Founder's anticipated 4/5 remain distinct from an actual rating of this execution.

## R-F v2 — State the loss and keep the accounting contrast

> SAVED $12.  
> LOST THE LAMP.  
> KEPT THE REGRET.

Essential visual for a later render: an empty-handed hunter at a flea-market stall as another buyer leaves with the SOLD-tagged lamp. The transaction-like three-line layout is dominant; scene small but legible.

E002/E011 support regret over a passed-over find. Lamp, amount, setting and exact staging are inventions; E002 supports a flea-market setting, E011's original venue was unspecified. Analysis plus formal-record/accounting exploration (TRM-002 parent recipe); preserve a visibly transaction-like layout if naming that recipe. This is a structural rewrite, not merely a replacement third sentence.

Provisional evaluation: saved/lost/kept links the financial benefit to the missed object and retained feeling. It states regret more clearly than “still paying for it,” but is more explicit and may sacrifice subtlety. “Lost” could imply former ownership unless the scene makes the missed purchase clear. No new 4/5 claim. Original R-F remains 3.5/5.
