# R012 — Type-Led Design 4

**Working name:** Thrill of the Find — Free Handwritten

**Primary assortment role:** Attention / Scroll-Stop

**Secondary role:** category connection through `ANTIQUES ARE WORTH THE DETOUR`

## Founder-approved execution

- Warm off-white / ivory garment.
- Female-leaning but still wearable as a unisex product.
- Main phrase: `Thrill of the Find` — no leading `The`.
- All main words use a bold, free, hand-written / hand-lettered treatment.
- The script intentionally keeps a steeper slant, larger gestures, thicker strokes, and more motion than the later restrained refinements.
- `OF THE` remains a compact linking phrase within the handwritten composition.
- Rust angled underline beneath `Find`.
- Small supporting line: `ANTIQUES ARE WORTH THE DETOUR`.
- Deep olive + rust palette.
- Composition prioritizes phone-thumbnail / social-scroll impact over typographic restraint.
- Front print only.
- The design is intentionally fashion-first and type-led: the expressive lettering itself is the visual event.
