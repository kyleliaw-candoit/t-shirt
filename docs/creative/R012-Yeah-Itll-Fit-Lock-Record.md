# R012 — Yeah, it'll Fit

**Creative mechanism:** broad behavioral humor / treasure-hunter logistics optimism

**Core recognition:** treasure hunters routinely convince themselves that an absurdly oversized find will somehow fit into the car.

## Shared locked visual decisions

- Controlled first-MVV execution on the warm off-white / ivory garment.
- Modern-clean illustration language rather than detailed vintage engraving.
- Tiny classic European-style compact car; it need not represent a literal branded Mini or Fiat.
- Absurdly oversized antique armchair creates the humor through comic proportion.
- No separate floating hatch/trunk door.
- No house-move clutter such as nightstand, lamp, plant, or unrelated household objects.
- Copy: `YEAH, it'll fit.`
- `YEAH,` uses dark condensed / block uppercase typography.
- `it'll fit.` uses a warmer handwritten/script treatment.
- Keep the graphic clean, wearable, and immediately legible at distance.
- Front print only.
