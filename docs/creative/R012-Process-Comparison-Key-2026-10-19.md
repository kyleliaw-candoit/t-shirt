# R012 — Creative-process comparison: protocol and process key

2026-10-19 · Exploratory trial · Results pending · Not a methodology amendment

**Spoiler:** This file reveals process assignments. Rate the [review cards](R012-Process-Comparison-Review-2026-10-19.md) before opening the key.

## Purpose and boundaries

Founder authorized a comparison across missed-find regret, transport constraints, collection completion and nostalgia. Compare direct recipe exploration (D), explicit material analysis then recipe exploration (X), and explicit material analysis then free drafting with no deliberate recipe requirement (F). Two selected concepts per approach per material: 24 review cards. The original ranking and approved audience/positioning remain unchanged. Collection completion remains the previously approved humor-led exception to recognizability order.

This is one author's exploratory writing exercise, not a randomized causal experiment or an independent-agent test. Existing recipe knowledge, previous concepts and Founder feedback are present throughout. F means no deliberate recipe selection or retrospective recipe-fit requirement, not absence of learned patterns. Starting instructions differ; cognitive purity cannot be verified. Equal candidate counts approximate effort, not measured equal time or compute. Process label concealment reduces one review cue but does not guarantee blinding: formats can reveal their origins. No reliability claim follows from this round alone.

## Equal working scope

For each approach/material cell, draft three directions, select two for review, and record the rejected third. Thus 36 directions considered and 24 selected. The cells were developed in a shared session, not isolated runs; order effects and cross-approach carryover remain limitations. No new source research or independent corroboration was performed. Candidate selections are editorial judgments, not results. Some finalists may be weak: do not force an advance in every cell.

D begins from the episode and recipe operations without a separately required tension inventory. X uses the explicit inventories below before recipe application. F uses those inventories without deliberately consulting a recipe for drafting. Plain text and essential visual directions are the common presentation standard; text-only and visual humor are not assumed equivalent in a future rendered test.

## Explicit material analysis for X and F

### Missed-find regret — E002 / E011

Passing on an object leaves no physical possession but can leave attachment; the time spent reconsidering is time another buyer can act; a useful purpose may occur to the finder after leaving. E011's partners wanted it before discovering it gone. Unavailability causing desire is an explicitly invented variant, not an established source fact.

### Transport constraints — E007

The object fits the buyer's taste but not the vehicle; purchase can precede the transport solution; the buyer can regard the car, rather than the find, as the wrong-sized thing. E007 involved a thrift-shop hat rack and help from a husband's truck, not spouse conflict. Cabinet substitution is invented.

### Collection completion — E004

Completion settles a checklist but not attraction; the newly encountered duplicate is unnecessary as a category yet desirable as an individual object. E004 supports continued desire, not addiction, debt, or endless buying.

### Nostalgia — E005

Ownership is new but familiarity is old; a stranger's object evokes one's own family; a price can buy the object without describing its personal meaning. E005 concerns replacement objects, not authenticated ownership by the buyer's relative.

## Review and interpretation plan

Present one group at a time with approach labels withheld and without persuasive explanations or predicted winners. Ask humor 1–5 (halves/ties allowed), advance yes/maybe/no, and optional warmth/recognition comments. Keep exact wording fixed during the first scoring pass. Rewrites after feedback are a later round, not replacements silently inserted into this comparison.

After all four groups are rated, reveal the key. For each approach show the eight individual ratings grouped by material, its best rating in each material, and the number of concepts receiving at least 4/5, alongside shirt-advance judgments. The 4/5 threshold is a proposed practical benchmark anchored to the current transport favorite, not a validated statistical cutoff. Note ties and conflicting criteria. Compare within material before pooling: nostalgia's warmth is not failed humor evidence for every use case. Do not infer the generation pool's hit rate from ratings of selected finalists.

Founder ratings supply taste calibration, not audience recognition or purchase intent. With two finalists per cell and one author, results can guide the next working round but cannot establish general superiority. Repeat on fresh materials if a meaningful advantage appears; preserve material-dependent results or no winner. No automatic methodology update, merge, visuals, live publication or market test is authorized by a favorable rating alone.
