## C01 — The non-purchase receipt

**Proposed shirt copy**
> FLEA MARKET RECEIPT  
> ITEMS PURCHASED: 0  
> ITEMS STILL THINKING ABOUT: 1

**Visual:** A compact receipt silhouette. One small line drawing of a distinctive, unbranded ceramic lamp at its bottom, with an empty checkbox. The three lines must carry the idea at shirt-viewing distance; no decorative fine print.

**Evidence:** E002/E011: returning too late and continuing to care about a find that was passed over. Flea market is documented in E002; the lamp and receipt are invented staging, not the original objects.

**Audience / recognition prerequisite:** Hunters who have mentally kept a find after physically leaving it. No appraisal expertise.

**TRACE:** [TRM-002 — formal record for an informal experience](trace/mechanisms/TRM-002-Formal-Record-Informal-Experience.md). A receipt normally accounts for purchased goods; here it records the mental residue of not buying. This is a parent-recipe application, not a newly scored mechanism.

**Intended turn / wearer value:** Leaving empty-handed still generated a lasting “transaction.” Affectionate self-recognition rather than a claim that everybody buys impulsively.

**Risk / kill condition:** If read as an ordinary budgeting receipt, or if “still thinking” produces no recognition, the mismatch is too weak. Keep it short; do not add years of obsession unsupported by the source.

## C02 — Great at letting go

**Proposed shirt copy**
> I'M GREAT AT LETTING GO.

**Visual:** A hunter walking away empty-handed beneath a huge thought bubble occupied by the same odd ceramic lamp, now carrying a SOLD tag. The posture is composed; the disproportionate thought bubble contradicts the declaration. Avoid gender cues and spouse-conflict stereotypes.

**Evidence:** E002/E011: initial restraint followed by regret. Lamp, person and visual exaggeration are invented; the source does not establish clinical obsession.

**Audience / recognition prerequisite:** People who recognize replaying a missed purchase. Must understand that the thought bubble is the speaker's continued attachment.

**TRACE:** [TRM-018 — favorable claim undercut by its pictured subject](trace/mechanisms/TRM-Catalog-004-025.md#trm-018). The favorable claim is emotional detachment; the speaker's visible preoccupation contradicts it.

**Intended turn / wearer value:** Physically letting go is easy; mentally letting go is another matter. Invites recognition of one's own small contradiction.

**Risk / kill condition:** A generic relationship-breakup reading may dominate. The odd find and SOLD tag are essential; if they require explanation, do not advance.

## C03 — Complete, with a sequel

**Proposed shirt copy**
> THE COLLECTION IS COMPLETE.  
> I'M WORKING ON THE SEQUEL.

**Visual:** A complete graduated set of four plain mixing bowls, followed by one duplicate bowl separated by a small gap. No branded pattern or logo. The separate duplicate is essential; this is not an incomplete set.

**Evidence:** E004: a duplicate yellow bowl remains tempting despite an already complete set. The exact arrangement and decision to build a “sequel” are invented comic exaggeration, not a participant quotation or reported plan.

**Audience / recognition prerequisite:** Set collectors who recognize that completion need not extinguish desire; familiarity with movie/book sequels. Narrower than all vintage hunters.

**TRACE:** [TRM-063 — category substitution makes an enthusiasm tangible](trace/mechanisms/TRM-Catalog-048-070.md#trm-063). A completed collection is treated as a finished work with a sequel. Continuation after an apparent ending supplies the bridge.

**Intended turn / wearer value:** Completion, supposedly the stopping point, becomes permission to begin the next installment. Affectionate self-justification tied to the actual duplicate-find situation.

**Risk / kill condition:** If “sequel” is merely a clever label rather than a laugh, rewrite or drop. Must not imply all collectors are compulsive or that financial irresponsibility is admirable.

## C04 — Ready for a bowl emergency

**Proposed shirt copy**
> EXCESSIVE  
> adjective  
> READY FOR A THREE-BOWL EMERGENCY.

**Visual:** Compact dictionary-entry layout with three identical plain bowls beneath it, one still carrying a sale tag. Use large readable text, no fake pronunciation or extra fine print.

**Evidence:** E004: difficulty passing up a duplicate after completing a set. The three-bowl emergency is invented exaggeration, not documented community language or a reported event.

**Audience / recognition prerequisite:** Collectors who distinguish owning an example from wanting this newly encountered example; recognizing a duplicate is essential.

**TRACE:** [TRM-028 — dictionary entry justifies an informal reputation](trace/mechanisms/TRM-Catalog-026-047.md#trm-028). The headword names the affectionate fault of excess; its definition recasts duplicate buying as admirable emergency preparedness. The definition format and self-serving reinterpretation are essential.

**Intended turn / wearer value:** A solemn definition treats an implausible shortage of bowls as the emergency that justifies owning duplicates.

**Risk / kill condition:** If the emergency sounds merely practical or the language feels judgmental toward collectors, rewrite or discard. Do not claim that the actual participant used this rationale.

## C05 — Failed fit assessment, approved purchase

**Proposed shirt copy**
> FIT CHECK  
> MY TASTE  ✓  
> MY CAR  ✗  
> I'LL TAKE IT.

**Visual:** A very simple oversized vintage hat rack next to a small open hatchback, with the compact checklist above. The item visibly cannot fit; do not depict unsafe driving or suggest a transport technique.

**Evidence:** E007: purchased thrift-shop hat rack required a truck because it would not fit the buyer's car. The checklist and exact staging are invented; do not relabel this evidence as a flea-market event.

**Audience / recognition prerequisite:** Furniture/decor/display hunters who have faced practical transport limits. Not a universal small-collectible experience.

**TRACE:** [TRM-002 — formal record for an informal experience](trace/mechanisms/TRM-002-Formal-Record-Informal-Experience.md). A suitability check normally helps reject an impractical choice; the verdict openly privileges emotional fit despite the failed physical criterion.

**Intended turn / wearer value:** The finder treats the decisive practical objection as merely one unchecked box. Celebrates a particular compelling find without glorifying financial irresponsibility.

**Risk / kill condition:** If it reads as generic shopping excess, the distinctive object and physical mismatch are not doing enough. The final verdict must be readable without a dense form.

## C06 — Emotional baggage, cast iron edition

**Proposed shirt copy**
> EMOTIONAL BAGGAGE

**Visual:** A walking hunter leans toward a low-hanging tote containing one comically heavy cast-iron eggplant. A small tag reads CAST IRON; a tiny heart sits beside the object. No additional explanation line. The object must look peculiar and loved, not like ordinary groceries.

**Evidence:** E012: a cast-iron eggplant was left behind because of carrying inconvenience and later regretted. E007 supports the separate experience of buying despite transport difficulty. This concept imagines choosing to carry the odd find; it does not claim E012's participant bought it.

**Audience / recognition prerequisite:** Odd-object hunters who understand attachment to a find and the physical cost of bringing it home; the familiar figurative phrase must also register.

**TRACE:** [TRM-004 — dual reading through a concrete cue](trace/mechanisms/TRM-Catalog-004-025.md#trm-004). “Baggage” is a literal carried load and a figurative emotional burden; affection for the heavy object connects them. Fit remains provisional if the image reads only as literal luggage.

**Intended turn / wearer value:** The emotional attachment has acquired an actual carrying weight. A visual pun about a specific odd find rather than generic love of shopping.

**Risk / kill condition:** Phrase is familiar and potentially crowded; no originality clearance claimed. If it reads as mental-health commentary, travel merchandise, groceries, or requires explaining the eggplant's weight, hold it.

**Humor-first steering, October 19:** The Founder explicitly prioritized humorous materials for this test while drafting was underway. Replace the two nostalgia-led proposals with E004 collection-completion material. Keep E005 in the ranked bank for later; do not retrospectively lower its recognition estimate. E004 ranks below E019, E009 and E003, but offers a distinct, source-backed contradiction instead of another missed-opportunity variation or an account primarily demonstrating restraint. This is a creative selection judgment, not evidence that E004 is funnier to customers.

C01, C03 and C05 are the initial text-first leads. C02 and C06 depend on their visuals; C04 is the most exaggerated rationale and should not advance just to fill a slot. These are six candidates for review, not six approved designs. No concept has been audience-tested.

**Founder review:** Does the premise produce a laugh or clear comic surprise without reading the explanation? Does it feel specific to a treasure hunter or collector? Would someone wear it? Warmth, familiarity and cleverness alone do not satisfy this round's humor priority. Choose advance, rewrite or drop; do not force a winner from every group.
