## How the grouping was made

All 220 complete retained images were inspected in eleven contact sheets. Representative anchors were enlarged, and source-record observations were checked. Grouping began with visible relationships, then used lightweight attributes to explain and retrieve those relationships. No numeric clustering model, embedding-distance threshold or demographic inference was used.

**80 references are marked core; 86 additional references are boundary-only; 54 remain outside these eight families.** Core/boundary membership may overlap between families. These counts describe this analyst's working map, not natural cluster sizes, market shares, quality scores or independent buyer votes. The [machine-readable log](review-log.json) preserves membership, source observations, image hashes and paths for every record.

An unassigned reference is still useful evidence. Examples include animal processions (ETSY-019/040/045), icon-only and patterned silhouettes (LIG-001/006/011/014), grids/diagrams (SNORG-001/006/017, RB-037), full illustration without this pass's recurring layout (RB-028/033/042/043/044), and photographic or abstract treatments. A later pass may surface another family from these. No design was removed merely because it did not fit the initial eight.

## What this changes in judgment

- **Architecture is not a complete recipe.** An arch/image/footer arrangement can feel engraved and collected, cheerful and cartoon-like, or collegiate. Type voice, edge treatment, palette and garment jointly determine the result.
- **Subject likeness is not design likeness.** RB-005 and SNORG-010 use the same crow-count premise but have different visual relationships. Conversely, aircraft, raccoons and wildflowers can share aspects of a heritage illustrated treatment.
- **Scale has several dimensions.** A shallow handwritten wordmark can span much of the chest and still feel quiet. Track width, height, visual density and surrounding cloth independently.
- **An image needs a semantic job.** For CAR: WRONG, a prominent antique cabinet and open small car communicate the purchase/transport situation. A tiny decorative icon fails that job even if the shirt looks tidy.
- **Garment is part of the recipe.** Trim, washed appearance, solid color, boxy/fitted silhouette and styling affect the result. Styled photos do not prove physical fit, fabric weight or print dimensions. Actual specifications remain attributed to the seller record where available.
- **A prompt is not successful execution.** The first playful CAR version retained engraved detail and an overemphasized setup. The revision and frozen diagnosis show how the recipe informed correction rather than serving as a label added afterward.

These are working observations and applications of the approved methodology. They do not introduce a new mandatory process gate or silently promote a family to validated status.

## Evidence and measurement limits

The sample contains Etsy 44, Redbubble 44, Zazzle 44, Life is Good 20, SnorgTees 20, HistoreeTees 20, Sloth Hiking Club 20 and The Bee & The Fox 8. Commercial proxies differ: Etsy listing-level Bestseller badges within a Relevancy discovery route; marketplace Best Selling/Popular routes; and seller-controlled bestseller collection membership. Sloth's collection was displayed in Featured order. These are not comparable audited sales ranks.

Season, query, platform, seller curation, repeated templates, subject appeal, garment variants and existing brand audiences can affect selection. Bee & The Fox supplies most of DLF-005's core evidence; that concentration lowers confidence in broad transfer. Women's/men's navigation does not establish actual buyer demographics or a universal aesthetic preference. Source admission and application-specific audience fit remain separate decisions.

Measurements are manual two-dimensional estimates from retained promotional images. Each card states its source, crop coordinates, denominator and uncertainty. Palette hex values are interpretive proxies, not measured production ink formulas. Neutral diagrams are independent structural sketches: they preserve selected relationships, while the source images and crops retain the finer style evidence. A sketch alone is not a complete style specification.

Front graphics are visible; unverified backs remain unverified. No source-record eligibility status is upgraded by this analysis. No physical garment, print test, cold-viewer comprehension test or purchase study has been completed.

## Recommended review and next experiment

First review the two CAR applications and their assessments, then challenge family membership or essential/flexible claims where the images disagree. Preserve Founder feedback as a new dated addition; do not overwrite the frozen assessment. After that, apply a promising family to a second material/concept and test an audience with a different aesthetic context. That is a stronger transfer test than repeatedly polishing this one cabinet joke. Keep willingness-to-wear and identity recognition separate from recipe compliance.

The original pilot and its pending DLR-002 v2 feedback remain intact. Kittl remains shelved. This round does not authorize production, a launch or a market test.
