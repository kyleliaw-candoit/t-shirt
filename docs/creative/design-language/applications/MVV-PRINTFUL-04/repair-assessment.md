# Independent repair assessment — before founder feedback

October 23, 2026. Founder authorized vector reconstruction and dedicated restoration/upscaling after the rejected image-generation trial. All nine previously under-resolution designs were processed. The original approved files and all previous frozen assessments remain unchanged.

## Decision

Advance these nine candidates to provider/sample evaluation. The three outlined vector masters resolve scalable-contour output; the six neural restorations improve edge presentation modestly and retain recognizable detail. They do not prove recovery of missing native detail or printed fidelity. Do not relabel the raster originals as native 300 PPI.

## Visual findings

- **Type-Led-4:** source-shaped script, slant, orange underline and small support line remain intact. Contours are cleaner; the palette is flatter and the finest brush texture slightly simplified. Unlike the previous generative trial, letters have not been reinterpreted into a different composition.
- **Type-Led-3:** the initial simplified trace removed too much distress and was rejected (retained comparison). The final trace retains small distress paths and a broader palette. It is close at shirt scale; microscopic texture still differs. Accept for a print sample, not as pixel-identical artwork.
- **Fashion-1D-F:** script, key, rays, supporting copy and asymmetric spacing remain consistent. Most visible distress survives. Tiny colour flecks are simplified into filled paths.
- **B2:** the sign, arrow, post and script remain unchanged in meaning and layout. Restoration retains rust marks and makes edges slightly firmer. Tiny rust specks remain sample-dependent.
- **A1:** carved chair silhouette, green cushion, rays and copy stay faithful. Carving detail receives modest sharpening, without new ornaments.
- **A3:** chair silhouette, rush-seat weave and copy remain faithful. Weave looks slightly firmer at enlarged inspection; it is reconstructed texture, not independently recovered original detail.
- **YIF-M / YIF-F:** cars, antique chairs, wording, palettes and proportions remain faithful; contours appear slightly cleaner. Pink/gold feminine cues remain in YIF-F.
- **C2:** tag edges, cord, lettering and BOOTH 42 remain faithful. The paper texture is slightly smoother, a tradeoff of conservative reconstruction and palette export.

Whole-shirt pairs, artwork pairs and enlarged detail crops were inspected. Actual saved-export proof was also inspected separately. No new aesthetic ranking or market claim is implied by the numeric fidelity metrics.

## Transparency and size

All nine final PNGs contain only fully opaque ink and fully transparent exterior/counters. Source alpha below 128 is omitted; near-opaque source ink is composited against the approved Ivory reference before being made opaque. This removes faint edge haze but ties colour matching to this Ivory execution. It is not a garment-colour-independent conversion and must be rechecked before using a dark garment. Some intended light texture is represented as opaque pale ink, not garment-show-through; physical samples must assess this.

The previous partial-alpha percentage counted values as high as 249 and included antialiased edges. It overstated the impression of broad, low-opacity fades. The new audit separately measures deeper interior alpha below 200; the issue is more localized than that earlier headline implied.

Approved placement rectangles and aspect ratios are retained. Actual ink bounds shift by less than 0.025 inch from noise removal/contour cleanup. There is no clipping. Every export is a 3600 × 4800 transparent PNG at 300 PPI with an sRGB profile. This is an export specification, not proof of native recovered detail. C3B is retained unchanged from its approved round03 version because its graphic/caption already clear 150 native PPI.
