## Physical test protocol

Photograph each shirt flat and worn, front and detail, in consistent daylight before washing. Measure actual ink width/height and distance from collar seam; compare with approved placement and provider proof. Record actual garment size and fit. Inspect at normal viewing distance and close-up for jagged contours, lost distress, blurry texture, halos, opaque background, colour mismatch and uncomfortable ink feel. Pay particular attention to the three features above.

Wash according to the delivered care instructions; record settings and photograph after washes 1 and 5 under the same conditions. Check fading, cracking, peeling, shrinkage and placement changes. A three-design test validates representative processes only, not every unprinted design. Escalate any design-specific uncertainty to an additional sample.
