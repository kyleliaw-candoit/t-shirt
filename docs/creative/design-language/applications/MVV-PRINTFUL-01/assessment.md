## Provider and size judgment

All ten uploaded layouts display Good / 150 DPI at 12 × 16 inches in Printful, and all ten generated previews were inspected. No visible clipping, collar collision or abdomen drift appeared. Ivory gives sufficient preview contrast. C3B is intentionally wider than the other designs; its four stages justify that footprint. The other layouts remain centered and restrained.

Visible artwork dimensions are distinct from the padded canvas. Measured native resolution is 151.86–178 PPI at the selected widths. Exporting at 150 DPI does not create new detail. Type-Led-4 has the least enlargement headroom; a larger version requires a higher-detail master.

The S/L/2XL comparison is a nominal garment-width calculation, not three verified physical mockup sizes. See layout-specs.json and provider-setup.md. Current 1717 guidance lists the same 12 × 16 area for all sizes; fixed-inch placement is the working inference from that and current Printful scaling guidance. The generic preview's physical garment size is unknown. Production placement and garment measurements have tolerances.

## Limits and release status

These are reconstructed transparent derivatives because the available locked assets were flattened boards/mockups. They preserve the concept and composition but do not reproduce every original letterform, texture or illustration pixel. The historical locked files remain unchanged. Founder approval of the original is not transferred to these new files.

The no-branding treatment is visually successful: WD and its lines are removed from Fashion-1D-F; the type-led brand sentence becomes ANTIQUES. VINTAGE. DISCOVERY.; compass stars and optional A1/A3 neck marks are excluded. Decorative YIF-F sparkles are not business identifiers.

Production release is not certified. Generated masters retain partial-alpha edge/distress pixels, including near-opaque ink. No automatic alpha cleanup was performed. Printful advises avoiding semitransparent graphics; its green resolution badge is not an opacity or fine-detail acceptance test. Before a production lock, resolve the opacity treatment and verify fine-detail retention with a physical sample. DTG colors, particularly C3B's bright labels, are not guaranteed fluorescent. No sample was ordered, and no sales-performance claim is made.
