# Independent assessment — round 02

October 23, 2026 (America/Chicago). Recorded before founder feedback on this revision. This is a local visual assessment; new Printful verification is blocked by its terms-acceptance gate.

## Judgment

Recommend these revised footprints and the half-inch downward shift. Round01 was too small across nine designs, and my prior assessment overvalued restraint and the available native raster resolution. The founder's correction identifies a real garment-level weakness: a readable isolated composition can still have insufficient presence on the shirt. Selecting print size to maintain a resolution badge was the wrong design tradeoff. Keep the desired visual scale and make production-master quality a separate task.

The revisions reuse the same artwork, color and aspect ratios. Medium is deliberately 8–9 inches wide depending on composition; the two female-oriented executions are large at 10.5 inches. C3B remains its established 10.5-inch-wide strip. These are this batch's choices, not universal gender or family rules.

## Placement evidence and limits

I reinspected the saved nine-reference front-placement study and its actual annotated board (HistoreeTees, Life is Good, Redbubble). It is a selected reference set, not proof that a position causes best-seller performance. Its central lesson is to judge collar breathing room and bottom edge together; its ratios are not a physical-inch prescription.

All artwork moves down 0.5 inches in print-canvas coordinates: top 2 inches, except C3B at 2.75. This is a modest visible shift and avoids a second overcorrection toward the abdomen. On the reused local geometry, the shift is about 9 image pixels at 602-pixel presentation size; the estimated lowest center collar edge is near y70, giving roughly 63 pixels to first ink for the usual anchor and 76 pixels for C3B. These are provisional image estimates, not verified physical neckline distances. The local whole-shirt board and individual before/after views were inspected; geometry and generic reference size remain uncalibrated.

Every layout fits 12 × 16 inches. Nominal S/L/2XL width comparisons are recalculated in layout-specs.json under the existing fixed-inch working inference. Largest width is 57.5% / 47.7% / 40.4% of the nominal flat garment widths respectively. These calculations do not verify worn drape.
