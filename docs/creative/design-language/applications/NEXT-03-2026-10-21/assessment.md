## N-A — Price: $8 / Sentimental value: Please don't tell the seller.

**Separate outcomes:** warm private recognition strong; dry humor moderate-to-strong; wearability strongest for customers who like collectible label/card graphics. It is a different emotional/comic turn from Saturday at Grandma's: the public price versus concealed private value. Founder reported 4.5/5 on the original concept; that is not a new numeric humor score for the shirt.

**Intent and relationships:** DLF-006 adapts as a dealer valuation card. Classic slate serif/rules, engraved used bowl and rust private-value answer form one artifact. The border does functional work, grouping ordinary price and extraordinary personal value; it is not merely decorative vintage framing. PRICE/$8 establishes the public record, while rust separates the secret reply. The bowl remains a large material-specific object rather than a tiny icon.

**Comprehension without research:** labeled SENTIMENTAL VALUE and the response communicate private attachment and a buyer/seller relationship without needing a family vignette. The plain striped bowl cues used household objects but cannot prove recognition by every vintage hunter. PRICE: $8 could initially be mistaken for the price of the shirt or a bargain claim; the bowl and second field favor the intended mock-valuation reading, but cold-viewer feedback should check that possibility. $8 is invented staging, not a commercial offer.

**Two scales and reference comparison:** close up, the fields/rules/bowl belong to a collected print system. At shirt scale, PRICE: $8 and the bowl read first; the longer private reply requires a closer look. The bottom is denser than the top, but lines do not merge in the inspected render. DLS-ETSY-016's integrated illustration/title/boundary and DLS-ZZ-029's open interior informed the card, without their distinctive artwork. Compared with Grandma B, this is object/record-led, not a second family-memory scene.

**Footprint/color choice:** prefer medium 8 × 9.9623 inches over compact 6.75 × 8.4057. The extra width improves the long lower field and reply; the compact looks neat but asks too much of small type. The medium remains a bounded chest card rather than a full-canvas poster. Ivory is clearly strongest for slate/rust contrast and warm bowl tones; the three darker colors lose essential copy. Keep one colorway.

**Limits / disposition:** advance for Founder review. The bowl's fine hatching and frame require production/sample judgment. Native effective detail at selected width is about 125.9 ppi; do not select the smaller print merely to make a resolution number look better at the expense of comprehension. Prepare a higher-native-detail derivative before release, preserving the composition. Current provider border-warp cautions also apply.

**Separate outcomes:** clear self-aware confidence; humor moderate, less of a surprise than T-E; quiet wearability plausible-to-strong. Founder rated the original concept 4/5. The exact compact rendering is not thereby approved or demand-validated.

**Intent and relationships:** DLF-002 quiet vignette is extended to one simple two-object relation: tagged antique wardrobe versus open small hatchback. V2 makes the heading narrower and lets the pair lead; cream/ochre on Blue Jean feels calmer than T-E's colored cartoon cluster. The car is more filled than the intended sparse contour treatment, but remains coherent with the wardrobe. The actual color-ground rendering does not show an obvious broad halo at shirt scale; edge opacity still needs production inspection.

**Comprehension without research:** the wardrobe cannot plausibly pass through the little rear opening; the sale tag and antique proportions support found-object context. The copy announces commitment and treats transport as a later problem. It does not require an explanation of the original hat-rack episode. The payoff is quieter than the delivery timing reveal, which is a meaningful difference in voice rather than automatically a defect.

**Two scales and reference comparison:** at detail scale, the open hatch and older cabinet are unambiguous. At whole-shirt scale, both silhouettes and the top commitment survive; the lower aside is the weakest read and needs conversational viewing distance. Compared with DLS-RB-039's isolated figure/aside and DLS-HISTOREE-013's figure/caption, this application requires more object information. It retains the open garment perimeter and plain voice without pretending to be a one-icon replica. Avoid shrinking until the relationship vanishes.

**Footprint/color choice:** prefer medium 9.25 × 7.8495 inches over compact 8 × 6.7887. It improves the lower caption and open hatch while retaining a shallow, open silhouette. Blue Jean makes a softer casual whole than Pepper; Pepper is also legible but does not earn a second colorway now. Moss is plausible; Ivory loses cream copy almost entirely. Keep one.

**Limits / disposition:** advance as the third candidate, with lower priority than T-E for immediate attention. Native effective resolution is about 122.8 ppi at selected width. Typography/edge cleanup and a higher-native-detail derivative remain production work. No further aesthetic variant is justified solely because this is quieter than T-E. In an eventual store, consider its overlap with both CAR: WRONG and T-E; the one/two cap applies to each concept but is not permission to ignore assortment-level repetition.
