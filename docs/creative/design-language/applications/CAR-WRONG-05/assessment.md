## Self-directed correction

V1 increased upper spacing but made CAR: WRONG heavier and left the lower gap less resolved. That would repeat an earlier Founder objection about losing the classic character. It was rejected before this recommendation. V2 opens both gaps and better retains a moderate serif voice. Two targeted attempts were sufficient; further variants would reopen accepted decisions without a concrete remaining aesthetic question.

## Reusable lesson

Observation: individually appropriate elements felt cramped together. Effect: the intended composed heritage character weakened. Correction: reduce/reposition the central group to open two gaps. Tradeoff: reduced illustration prominence and finer physical detail. The whole-shirt review is necessary to judge that tradeoff; an enlarged artwork alone is insufficient.

Playful's dense treatment remains an accepted contrasting example. Preserve the specific rationale rather than generalizing “heritage = more whitespace” or “playful = crowding.”
