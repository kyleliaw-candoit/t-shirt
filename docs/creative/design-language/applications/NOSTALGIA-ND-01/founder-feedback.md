# Founder feedback and disposition — NOSTALGIA-ND-01

October 21, 2026 (America/Chicago) · Appended after the [frozen Creative Director assessment](assessment.md). Original artwork, prompts and assessment remain unchanged.

## Feedback

Founder agrees B looks better and sees nothing wrong with A. Someone can like the same wording while preferring A's presentation. The Creative Director's people-in-bowl concern remains an interpretation, not a confirmed defect and not a reason to force an A redesign.

Founder distinguishes two contributors to performance: creative concept and design. When two executions have no obvious issues, customer clicks/sales should inform which performs better; both may perform equally well. This fits the AI-powered optimization business model. Limit the storefront to **one or two visual designs per creative concept** to avoid excessive variations and choice overload. This is a working assortment decision, not a measured choice-paralysis effect or a quota to publish two.

Founder also accepts this round as evidence that the existing design principles/workflow transfer to a new concept and authorizes merging the reviewed work. This is practical internal workflow evidence across CAR: WRONG and N-D; it does not certify every future application, native artwork quality, physical fit, customer demand or provider fidelity.

## Decision

- Advance **both A Heritage / Ivory and B Framed artifact / Pepper** as store candidates; B is the preferred lead presentation, not a declared sales winner.
- Preserve both selected masters. Do not revise A merely to satisfy the Creative Director's earlier concern or produce more versions of this concept.
- Retain one selected colorway per design for now. Colorways are separate from the one/two visual-execution limit; avoid using them to recreate clutter.
- Complete the documented native-detail/transparency production work and final Printful preview check before store release. The browser failure remains a verification gap; aesthetic acceptance and documentation merge do not remove it.
- When testing is assigned, compare behavior with exposure, placement, price, presentation and traffic recorded. Click response and purchase response answer different questions; both designs can earn retention. This review does not authorize ad spend, a market test or store publication.

## Reusable operating lesson

The workflow produced two distinguishable, acceptable treatments of a second concept, and Founder agreed with the Creative Director's relative preference while declining to treat the alternate as defective. That supports continuing the current workflow without adding another methodology-development gate. Preserve the independent judgment, subsequent disagreement and eventual behavioral evidence separately.
