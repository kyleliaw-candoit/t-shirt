# Creative Director assessment — round 03

Recorded October 21, 2026, before Founder feedback on these revised assets. These are aesthetic judgments, not customer purchase evidence.

## What improved

Separating the art from garment rendering solves the repeatability problem that a prompt alone did not solve: the complete design can now be placed at an explicit physical footprint without regeneration. Both footprints leave substantially more garment space than round 02. The small generic open-hatch car and tagged cabinet preserve the material/identity cues.

The Playful draft has more consistent line weight and a clearer light-colored payoff. The Heritage draft retains its warm typographic character while avoiding a recognizable Mini Cooper and highly engraved rendering.

## Remaining design judgments

- **Comprehension:** the cabinet/open-hatch mismatch is understandable without the research explanation. The tag helps indicate a purchase, though it is a secondary cue and will become harder to read at compact scale. First-time viewer testing remains useful.
- **Semantic hierarchy:** width and height have equal weight in the shared setup. Heritage still gives “PERFECT” substantial emphasis; it competes more with the payoff than in Playful. This is a remaining refinement, not a claim that equal setup weights alone settle hierarchy.
- **Illustration:** Heritage is simpler than round 02 but retains unnecessary car trim, cabinet curls and gradients. Further reduce these in production artwork. Playful has clearer silhouettes, though its cream ground shapes and heavy outlines make the composition denser than necessary.
- **Audience fit:** muted clay, cream and dusty blue/teal fit the vintage-shopping context better than the previous bright treatment. These colors should not be assumed to fit all identities or audiences.
- **Print readiness:** neither raster master passes native-resolution review at the proposed dimensions. Printful's rendered-canvas quality badge does not override this finding.

## Placement and color decision

Compact is the preferred starting footprint for restrained wearability: 8.5″ wide, about 7.2–7.5″ tall. Medium at 10″ remains a useful alternative when first-glance recognition of the cabinet/tag needs more prominence. The medium is not rejected merely because it is larger; the relevant tradeoff is comprehension versus garment coverage, especially S versus 2XL.

All four 1717 trial colors were inspected in Printful with the artwork unchanged. Proposed colorways:

| Design | Put forward | Judgment on other trial colors |
|---|---|---|
| A Heritage | **Ivory, Pepper** | Ivory gives the warm cabinet/payoff good separation; Pepper provides a quieter dark alternative. Blue Jean brings the car closer to the blank color. Moss works but does not add enough over the two proposed options. |
| B Playful | **Pepper, Moss** | Pepper gives cream lettering strong emphasis; Moss supports the earthy palette. Blue Jean is credible but adds less distinct value. On Ivory, cream text loses its filled-letter emphasis and reads more like outlined lettering; do not offer unchanged merely to add a light option. |

These choices concern this artwork, not a general ranking of garment colors. The provider does not identify the actual garment size of its generic preview, so size-chart percentages are kept separate from visual mockup claims.

## Next decision

Choose the footprint and one or two garment colorways with Founder feedback; then clean/rebuild the selected artwork, resolve provider reference-size/fulfillment geometry, and inspect a physical sample before production. Do not use a low-resolution draft as a final Printful production file.
