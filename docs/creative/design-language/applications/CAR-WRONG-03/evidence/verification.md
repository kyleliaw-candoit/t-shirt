Observed October 21, 2026 in the public 1717 Design Maker, DTG front placement, US catalog context. No login, product publication or order was needed for these review previews.

## Observed

- Accepted the upload conditions only after the Founder explicitly authorized accepting Printful's terms and indemnification clause in this conversation.
- The 1717 front/back DTG table showed 12 × 16 inches for all sizes. The same page contains a generic scaling footnote; preserve that limitation rather than treating a template as a physical-print guarantee.
- The size guide showed nominal flat widths S 18.25, L 22, 2XL 26 inches, and lengths 26.625, 29.375, 31.625 inches. Measurement tolerance was stated as up to 2 inches.
- A-medium was applied as a 12 × 16 inch full canvas. S, L and 2XL were separately selected (checked via the visible size button's active class); the layer panel retained 12.00 × 16.00 units in each case. `A-medium-{S,L,2XL}-controls.jpg` preserve the corresponding selection screens. The visible artwork inside that canvas remains 10.00 × 8.52 inches by construction.
- All four application canvases loaded at 12.00 × 16.00 units. Each used the same full-canvas structure; narrower/larger visible artwork is encoded in its transparent layout, not inferred from a garment-size label.
- Pepper, Ivory, Blue Jean and Moss were selectable and rendered for both compact drafts. Variant stock across every size/region was not audited.
- Four flat provider previews (two designs × two footprints) and eight draped design previews (two designs × four colors) were visually inspected and retained.

## Limits

- The actual garment size used in the provider's generic mockup was not identified. The selected order-size control alone is not evidence that the photograph changes size. The size check includes verified editor dimensions plus a separate calculation using the size guide; a verified size-specific visual/drape check remains outstanding.
- Canvas export at 150 pixels/inch causes Printful to show “Good / 150 DPI.” The artwork's effective native resolution is lower. See the specs; none of these files is cleared for production.
- Uploads were performed in a guest session. The repository package is the durable review record; no claim is made that a saved template exists in the Founder's Printful account.
