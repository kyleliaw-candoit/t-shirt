## Starting checkpoint

Start with 20 distinct eligible designs per core source, for 160 source-level slots across eight sources:

- Etsy
- Life is Good
- SnorgTees
- HistoreeTees
- The Bee & The Fox
- Sloth Hiking Club
- Redbubble
- Zazzle

SimplyCuteTees' four preserved examples remain supplementary references; do not force a larger core quota. Boredwalk is deferred.

Twenty is a practical discovery checkpoint, not a statistically validated sample size. Identify tentative visual families during collection. After the checkpoint, add batches of ten selectively where new visual relationships continue appearing or family boundaries need clarification. Pause a source when additional examples mostly repeat captured relationships; no automatic requirement to reach thirty everywhere.

## Collection rules

- Follow the displayed bestseller/popularity ordering and record route, sort, position, date, exclusions and commercial signal. Zazzle Popular is a popularity proxy, not verified sales ranking; brand collections are seller-designated signals. Preserve Etsy listing-level badge evidence and discrepancies.
- For Redbubble and Zazzle, begin with ten distinct eligible designs from each of the men's and women's routes, deduplicating across routes. These are shopping categories, not verified buyer demographics. Redbubble fallback navigation: homepage → T-shirts → Women's or Men's T-shirts → Best Selling.
- Require front-only graphics. Exclude back-print designs, including known back logos; missing views do not prove a blank back.
- Exclude customization-dependent designs needing a name, photograph or other personal content to be complete. An optional editing button alone does not disqualify a finished predefined design.
- Retain existing licensed-character/celebrity exclusions. Illustration-only designs are eligible for this visual-language study.
- Deduplicate artwork and color/cut variants. Preserve useful garment differences without counting them as separate designs. Track cross-source duplicates so 160 source-level slots are not misreported as 160 globally unique artworks.
- Preserve the original curated discovery set. Count its designs toward the ordered sample only where they occur in that sample; otherwise retain them as supplementary contrast references.

## Interpretation and next stage

Retain source images, provenance, garment facts and lightweight visual attributes. Compare typography, illustration, composition, color, print scale and graphic–garment relationships, distinguishing visual likeness from shared subject matter.

Keep observed source context separate from audience-transfer hypotheses: an aesthetic may fit some antique/vintage treasure hunters yet fit Asian-food identities poorly. Recurrence is not a taste score or proof that visual treatment caused sales.

After assessing coverage, refine provisional families, create annotated cards and neutral reconstructions, then apply contrasting suitable families to the same audience/material/concept. The immediate next action is to address the Founder's question before any expanded scan begins.
