[Alternate source view](assets/DLS-BEE-001-1.jpg)

**Visible relationships:** Rounded heavy serif, two compact centered lines, black on cream, no illustration.

**Garment:** Fitted crewneck; 100% cotton; white or natural options (seller-listed).

**Source context:** Motherhood identity; retro-casual styling visible.

**Transfer hypothesis:** Short self-identification could suit a retro-oriented subgroup. Food or hunting context must be clear in original wording or a new cue; this source does not establish either audience’s taste.

**Limits:** Hair overlaps the print; tucked/layered styling is not a crop specification.
