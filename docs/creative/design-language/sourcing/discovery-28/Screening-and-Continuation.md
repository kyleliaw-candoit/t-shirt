## Evidence limits that affect interpretation

Collection membership and a platform badge are different commercial proxies. Neither isolates the effect of design language from subject, brand, price, garment or distribution. No actual gender split was established for any source. Regional or women-oriented positioning is context, not a universal taste category.

Front placement is visibly established. When no back view exists, the record does not certify a blank back. Known back graphics are excluded, and no large back art has been treated as a front source. Folded/tucked images cannot establish full torso proportions. Mockups cannot verify fiber, fit, feel, ink process or production quality. Source-image dimensions are file pixels, not physical print dimensions.
