## What is complete, and what remains

Every slot has an inspected front graphic, a source record, a retained image, design/garment observations, source context, a transfer hypothesis and limits. Known back-print sources are excluded, including two attractive candidates discovered to have small back logos. Where no back view exists, we do not certify a wholly unprinted garment; admission is based on a visibly confirmed front treatment, consistent with the agreed rule.

The six brand routes use seller-designated bestseller collections. Etsy uses item-level badge observations, not search keywords, Star Seller or store-wide sales. **ETSY-004 has conflicting page versions: an initially retrieved version displayed Bestseller; a later version showed Etsy’s Pick without Bestseller.** It is retained as dated commercial evidence, not claimed as a persistent current bestseller. Collection position is a relocation aid, never a sales rank. No exact sales, gender split, repeat purchase or cause of demand is known.

This batch is the source layer for image-native discovery. The candidate-relationship labels are retrieval notes, **not 28 recipes or confirmed clusters**. No new neutral reconstruction, annotated crop set, measured print specification or target-audience validation is claimed here. Existing DLR-001/002 and their CAR: WRONG applications remain unchanged.

## Recommended next execution

1. Compare the images across sellers and propose a small number of fuzzy families, keeping mixed and boundary examples. Separate likeness of subject from likeness of visual relationships.
2. For each promising family, make a visual card with full references, annotated details, labeled approximate relationships and an independent neutral reconstruction. State essential versus flexible relationships and garment constraints.
3. Apply two contrasting suitable families to the same audience/material/concept. Judge comprehension, identity connection, aesthetic fit, semantic hierarchy and willingness to wear before Founder feedback; then preserve the feedback and revisions.

Pay particular attention to text-only identity clarity, patterned silhouettes versus detail loss, quiet small prints versus narrative needs, and rectangular art that could drift toward a poster. Brand recognition, topic affinity and the garment itself remain competing explanations for source appeal.
