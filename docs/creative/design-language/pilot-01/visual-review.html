Two goals, two review questions

  1. Recipe usefulness: Do the original executions visibly follow the relationships decoded from the sources while expressing our own concept?
  2. Design judgment: Which shirt would you actually prefer to wear, and does the vintage-hunting situation register without an explanation?

Rate visual appeal, audience clarity and wearability separately, or simply choose advance / revise / hold. No need to reward a recipe for faithfulness if the shirt itself is unattractive.
