# HISTOREE-002 — HistoreeTees humor mechanisms, first five bestseller pages

2026-10-19 · Creative Director · TRACE v0.4.2 · Proposed shared-library additions, awaiting Founder merge approval.

## Result and business implication

The prior [HISTOREE-001 study](https://github.com/kyleliaw-candoit/t-shirt/blob/docs/trace-v03-calibration/docs/creative/trace/brand-studies/HISTOREE-001-Bestseller-Analysis-2026-10-03.md) inventoried 16 shirts, closely screened seven and produced **zero recipes** under the historical protocol. It did not complete this humor-decoding assignment. Its findings remain historical and unchanged.

This new study inspected the supplied collection's first five pages: **80 displayed T-shirts, 80 distinct product URLs, all 80 linked product-card images inspected**. It produced **53 candidate source interpretations**, mapped to **six existing recipes and 21 proposed new recipes (TRM-072–092)**. One source joins two recipes; recipe memberships must not be summed as unique shirts. If merged, the active candidate catalog grows from 68 to **89**, excluding the two broad parent/teaching cards and historical redirect. These are revisable creative operations, not scientifically independent mechanisms or proven sales effects.

Your humor hypothesis is productive as a research direction: this sample supplies several operations not represented by the current narrow Etsy recipes. It does **not** establish that humor outperforms other approaches. We sampled one history-focused merchant's seller-ranked products, not randomized audiences or sales outcomes.

The particularly useful addition is **reader inference**: a small caption becomes funny because the reader supplies the missing context. Historical knowledge is the context here; future original applications may use other shared audience knowledge. Dark subject matter is common in these examples but is not a requirement for every abstract operation, nor a new brand direction.

Source context must not become a fact claim through recipe reuse.

## Capture and evidence strength

Frame fixed before decoding: supplied [Greatest Hits](https://historeetees.com/collections/best-sellers-1) pages 1–5 in displayed order, all 16 slots per page, one seller, no replacement beyond the frame. Page/slot and source URLs are in the [evidence ledger](HISTOREE-002-Evidence.json). Direct public HTML was retrieved on October 19 and showed **Best selling selected on all five pages**. Collection name/order is merchant-controlled; ranking time window, units, paid exposure, persistence and item-review attribution were not established. Thus commercial evidence is contextual and weaker than attributable item sales. A single seller cannot demonstrate the multi-seller evidence labels in TRACE.

Search-service extracts contained older counts and minor order differences (including the Roanoke/relationship-letter order and tour title). The directly retrieved collection HTML and linked images control this roster. We did not silently splice cached lists into current order. No product-detail-page or review-widget inspection is claimed: this was collection-card image decoding.

The retained [page 1](HISTOREE-002-evidence/page-1.jpg), [page 2](HISTOREE-002-evidence/page-2.jpg), [page 3](HISTOREE-002-evidence/page-3.jpg), [page 4](HISTOREE-002-evidence/page-4.jpg) and [page 5](HISTOREE-002-evidence/page-5.jpg) contact sheets contain the observed product images in capture order with source IDs. They are research evidence, not production assets. The ledger retains individual image URLs and SHA-256 hashes plus page HTML hashes. Raw HTML and individual downloaded image files were temporary working captures; the contact sheets and structured observations are the retained evidence. Small text that was not reliably read was not invented.

All 80 URLs and displayed expressions are distinct within this frame. This does not prove independent authorship; repeated phrase scaffolds and same-merchant variants may be related. The study does not claim external spread or increase Etsy seller counts. Existing scores remain a frozen description of their original sample.
