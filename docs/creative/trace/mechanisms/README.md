## Founder clarification: overlap is welcome

The Founder reviewed the twelve concept sketches and found that they followed their mechanisms. **A concept derived from mechanism A does not need to be distinctive to A or ineligible under B.** Record the intended derivation and check whether the claimed relationship is present. Add secondary mechanism/family tags when useful. Do not reject, weaken a usability verdict or demand a rewrite merely because another mechanism also explains the concept.

Neighbor notes explain what a recipe requires; they are creative aids, not exclusivity tests. Originality relative to source wording/art remains a separate downstream concern. Founder feedback on mechanism consistency does not establish customer response or approve production.

## Four distinct records

| Record | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Family (F01–F11 here) | Provisional navigation grouping. May overlap, split or merge; eleven is not a permanent limit. |
| Recipe (TRM-001 etc., versioned) | Reusable operation plus essential ingredients and applicability guidance. One recipe can link several sources and families. |
| Source design (scan name + source number + listing ID) | A specific observed expression with platform, seller, commercial signal and interpretation. |
| Repeated commercial expression | Same or near-identical expression appearing in multiple offers. Distribution/recurrence is not a new independent recipe or corroboration. |

The sixty selected designs are not sixty independent mechanisms. Preserve source evidence and interpretation history when linking a source to a shared recipe.

## Card standard

1. Stable TRM ID, version, plain-language operation and proposed payoff.
2. Two to four concrete essential conditions; adjustable inputs; optional execution, specifying when an execution cue is essential.
3. Fits when / fails when, a short creation procedure and helpful neighboring operations. No exclusive-mechanism requirement.
4. Direct source-decode links, how visible details support the interpretation, and the distinctive source combination to avoid copying.
5. One illustrative application, clearly separate from observed commercial evidence. It may be an unvalidated hypothesis; target-community success is not a TRACE admission gate.
6. Separate mechanism-explanation confidence, source commercial evidence and downstream usability. Preserve maturity labels independently.
7. Revision record explaining meaningful changes.

Do not fill Required by repeating the mechanism sentence. Ask what relationships must actually survive a new execution. Do not blanket-label color, typography, labels or arrangement optional: package color can enable source 45; restraint carries the tone of 52; multiple readable correspondences support the taxonomy in 38.

## Using a recipe with an identity group

Read the current identity/brand context, state the relevant audience truth or assumption, and ask whether the recipe's applicability conditions are present. Then create a fresh scene or wording that preserves the essential relationship. Check that the intended relationship is perceptible and the tone fits. Multiple mechanisms can participate in the result.

Specificity to an identity group is a downstream creative judgment; **exclusivity to a mechanism is not required**. Illustrative applications do not supply independent source evidence, global originality clearance or customer validation.

## Incorporating future scans

Decode each eligible design individually first. Compare its actual operation and essential conditions with existing TRM cards, not merely matching words, characters or family labels.

- If the operation fits an existing recipe, add a traceable source link and explain the fit. Preserve platform-specific evidence scope in its source record.
- If the same expression recurs, record recurrence instead of a new independent example.
- If new conditions materially refine a recipe, version the card and state what changed. Recheck credited examples before strengthening any claim.
- If the operation is materially different, create a new stable TRM ID and link relevant neighbors. Do not force it into one of eleven families.
- If families or recipes split/merge, retain old IDs as redirects or superseded entries with the reason and destination; never silently renumber.

Use separate review dimensions. A clear explanation may have weak commercial evidence; a bestseller may have an uncertain mechanism interpretation; a usable concept exercise is not market validation. No evidence upgrade follows automatically from a new platform or a second seller.
