# TRM-003 — Shape correspondence across categories

**In plain language:** make one familiar shape readable as something from another category, so the viewer recognizes both in the same construction.

**Creative payoff hypothesis:** delight at a discovered resemblance, or recognition of a habit of looking closely. The audience relevance must come from the chosen categories and relationship.

## Essential ingredients

1. A genuine shared geometric feature, such as contour, curvature or proportion.
2. Recognizable cues from both categories retained in the resulting shape.
3. A transformation that the image demonstrates; a caption must not be the only evidence of resemblance.
4. If presenting a system or taxonomy, at least two distinct, readable correspondences. One successful pair can express the basic operation but cannot establish a multi-example system.

**Adjustable:** categories, number of pairs, arrangement and illustration style, provided the dual reading survives.

**Optional:** labels, texture and decorative framing. **Not optional:** readable geometry and, for a claimed taxonomy, multiple successful mappings. Labels may aid identification but cannot rescue arbitrary shapes.

## Identity fit and creation

**Fits when:** the audience recognizes both categories and the visual relationship expresses a valued behavior, tension or perspective. Merely combining two popular interests does not establish identity relevance.

**Fails when:** an animal wears a food costume without sharing its shape, or arbitrary squiggles need a paragraph to explain what they represent. Those images might use other mechanisms, but do not demonstrate this mapping.

Start with two recognizable categories connected to the identity context. Find real contour correspondences before writing copy. Sketch the strongest pair, then additional pairs if presenting a series. Inspect the images at shirt-viewing scale: can both categories be recognized with only brief labels? If not, revise the geometry rather than add explanatory prose.

**Neighboring operations:** visual metaphor, category substitution and causal fantasy scenes. They can overlap. A combined scene is acceptable when the claimed shape relationship is visible; no exclusivity test applies.

## Source grounding and copying boundary

[Etsy source 38 — Pasta pups](../pilots/TRACE-Etsy-60-Additional-Recipes.md#38--pasta-pups), listing 4575837353, shows dachshund bodies taking the shapes of named pasta varieties. Several labeled examples support the systematic shape-mapping interpretation.

**Avoid copying:** the labeled dachshund-as-pasta chart, its particular silhouettes, pasta/dog pairings and arrangement. Replacing dachshunds with another pet while retaining the same food taxonomy and visual construction may remain a close derivative. Choose a materially new category relationship and construction.

This single source supports CANDIDATE status. Outside offers of the same Pasta Pups expression do not supply independent mechanism applications.

## Illustrative application — not source evidence

**Remote-working parents:** calendar rectangles become generic toy building blocks across a small series, retaining day-grid cues. A school-pickup block bridges work blocks. Shared geometry makes arranging paid-work and parenting commitments visible.

This is review sketch C3. Its applicability assumes an audience familiar with school scheduling and children's building toys; it is not a universal portrait of remote parents. Avoid suggesting effortless simultaneous childcare and work, and avoid proprietary toy geometry.

The Founder found the review sketches consistent with their mechanisms. However, a written description does not establish that the two categories will actually read in the drawing. This remains a visual execution hypothesis, not approved production art, verified originality or customer evidence.

## Three separate assessments

- **Explanation confidence:** high for the recorded source's repeated animal/pasta shape relationships; no new image inspection in this documentation pass.
- **Commercial evidence:** source-specific Etsy signal and recurrence notes; no design-sales total or causal attribution.
- **Downstream usability:** concept generation demonstrated in prose; visual legibility remains unresolved until sketches are rendered and inspected. This execution uncertainty does not invalidate the recipe's library entry.
