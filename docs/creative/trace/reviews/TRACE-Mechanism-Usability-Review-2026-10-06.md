## What worked and what needs correction

The report correctly names these eleven groups **navigation families** and explicitly rejects interchangeability. Keeping the individual decodes is valuable: a reader can recover the difference between a speaker reveal, contradiction and deadpan shock.

The main usability defect is in the recipe handoff. In all 55 new cards, Required repeats the proposed mechanism sentence. The Observed ingredient cues and Adjustable fields contain useful information, but the reader must decide which cues are indispensable. Replace the repetition with two to four concrete conditions. For example, card 42 needs an animal trait, a task benefiting from that trait, and visible use of the trait in doing the task. An octopus merely posing beside drums would fail that operation.

Other concrete corrections:

1. Give reusable recipes stable, platform-independent IDs and versions; keep source numbers as source references. The family table needs direct links to recipes, not only numeric lists.
2. Give each recipe one fit condition and one failure/nonfit example. This is abstract guidance, not a demand for new community research before library entry.
3. Distinguish family, recipe, source design, and repeated commercial expression. The 60 selected designs are not 60 independent mechanisms or corroborations.
4. Replace generic copying warnings with the distinctive combination to avoid. Examples: card 38's labeled dachshund-as-pasta chart; card 49's dawn rally plus frog holding a found lamp; card 53's likes-list ending in a tiny numbered human allowance.
5. Scope optional execution correctly. In card 45, garment color can carry package recognition. In 52, understated presentation is part of the operation. In 38, multiple readable correspondences matter. None should be casually removed under generic optional styling language.
6. Separate mechanism explanation confidence, source commercial evidence, and downstream usability. A clear recipe can have weak external corroboration, and a bestseller can have an uncertain mechanism interpretation.
7. Permit secondary family tags. Use a primary family for navigation without suggesting mutually exclusive mechanisms: 46 strongly overlaps animal/creature humanization; 35 also borrows a glossary format; 58 also reframes nuisance as competence.
8. Keep borderline interpretations 07, 16, 29, 41, 45, 47 and 52 visible and revisable. They are not failures merely because they are quiet, single-source, or hard to transfer.

## Deep test 1 — Expectation mismatch: delayed reversal

**Source grounding:** 10 promises help then reverses the outcome; 53 begins with ordinary likes then adds a limiting human qualification. These support a broad delayed reinterpretation, but are separate source recipes. The exercise below tests the shared operation rather than claiming one newly corroborated recipe.

**In plain language:** get the reader to accept an ordinary premise, then reveal information that changes what the premise meant.

**Essential:** understandable setup; a delayed second beat; a recognizable connection that reinterprets the first; an identity-relevant payoff. **Adjustable:** speaker, situation, verbal versus sequential visual reveal. **Optional:** mascot or illustration. **Boundary:** simultaneous claim/image contradiction need not have a delayed reveal; random shock does not necessarily reinterpret a setup.

### Antique/vintage treasure hunters

Fit: the brief describes quick stops becoming long hunts and impractical finds creating transport problems. Hypothesis: affectionate self-awareness about those habits feels wearable. Nonfit: implying dishonesty, financial recklessness, or that every hunter hoards.

- **A1 — The shopping list.** An otherwise blank checklist reads “Nothing needed.” A second line, visually discovered after the first, reads “That wasn't the question.” A small hand-drawn booth number anchors the choice in discovery rather than generic consumption. The turn changes shopping from needs fulfillment to recognition of an unforeseen find. Risk: without the booth cue, it becomes generic shopping humor.
- **A2 — A measured decision.** “Measured twice.” Below, a tiny car outline with an object far too large for it: “Bought it anyway.” The second beat overturns the competence premise through certainty about the find. This is a new logistics scene, not a source character/caption replacement. Risk: common phrasing and possible prior use require downstream originality checking.

### Remote-working parents

Fit: internal research describes visible role changes, home-office interruptions and school pickup. Hypothesis: the wearer enjoys acknowledging the collision without disparaging either role. Nonfit: shaming childcare choices or implying parents are incapable workers.

- **A3 — An agenda addition.** A clean meeting agenda has two normal tasks; the last line changes to “Explain why the banana broke.” The final task reveals the parent behind an apparently ordinary workday. Use a very short three-line composition, not a full corporate slide. Risk: young-child specificity and weak recognition outside that stage.
- **A4 — A shorter commute.** “My commute is twelve steps.” Smaller second beat: “Today, six of them were LEGO.” An abstract path from desk to kitchen supplies the home-work setting without brand logos. The reveal reverses ease into a familiar domestic obstacle. Risk: branded word unnecessary in production; replace with “toy bricks” if advanced, and avoid copying branded toy geometry.

**Test outcome:** PASS for understanding the operation and producing distinct narrative hypotheses once the recipe is narrowed. **REVISE current family handoff:** the index does not tell a reader to preserve the delayed reinterpretation rather than merely add a surprising second line. Concepts have not been shown to customers.

## Deep test 2 — Borrowed formats: relocate an institutional form

**Source grounding:** 05 imports an award, 23 a warning, 27 a review, 32 a certificate, 45 a package and 55 an advertisement. The exercise transfers a recognizable form's authority to an unexpected personal situation.

**In plain language:** make an ordinary experience speak in the format of a system that normally describes something else.

**Essential:** recognizable format cues; unexpected subject or application; meaningful mismatch; audience-specific content. **Adjustable:** system, subject and emotional tone. **Optional:** simulated age or texture. **Boundary:** ordinary decoration resembling a form is insufficient; unlike simple value reframing, the recognizable format helps deliver the meaning.

### Antique/vintage treasure hunters

Fit: source brief values discovery and the story, not only ownership. Hypothesis: mock documentation of the hunt feels affectionate. Nonfit: fake appraisal authority or ornate auction-house formality that implies elite status.

- **B1 — Find arrival board.** A compact transport-style arrival board lists “Destination: home / Arrival: eventually / Delay: maker's mark investigation.” The travel system documents a hunt interruption. Its distinctive operation is the timetable accounting for discovery, not a decorative vintage ticket. Risk: too much text; retain only the strongest two fields.
- **B2 — Space application.** A plain household form: “Request for shelf space / Item: unexpected / Reason: look at it.” A tiny circled approval marks the emotional decision masquerading as administration. The subject is the finder's justification, not a claim of financial value. Risk: can imply uncontrolled accumulation; tone should celebrate one compelling find.

### Remote-working parents

Fit: research describes calendar conflicts and active boundary management. Hypothesis: administrative language captures the competence and absurdity of role-switching. Nonfit: treating children as disposable coworkers or celebrating permanent availability.

- **B3 — Doorway border control.** A small passport-style stamp reads “WORK → HOME,” with a crossing distance of “one doorway.” Paired desk and backpack cues make the role transition physical. An invented stamp system, no state emblems or real government seals. Risk: generic WFH unless backpack/parent context is legible.
- **B4 — Release notes.** A compact software-style notice: “Afternoon update / Meeting completed / Snack request reopened.” The record treats a recurring parenting demand as an unresolved system task. Keep warm, not resentful. Risk: software convention familiarity; works better for some professional segments than all remote parents.

**Test outcome:** PASS for the basic documentation's creative usability, with added explicit fit conditions. Four distinct format/subject combinations were possible without copying the source warning, one-star review or award phrase. These are concept sketches, not proof of global novelty.

## Deep test 3 — Visual recombination: shape correspondence across categories

**Source grounding:** 38 creates multiple dachshund/pasta shape correspondences. This tests that specific operation, not causal skeleton scenes 33/36 or departure metaphor 50.

**In plain language:** let one set of familiar shapes become another set, so the viewer can read both categories.

**Essential:** genuinely shared visible geometry; both categories still recognizable; at least two clear transformations if claiming a system/taxonomy; identity relevance beyond combining two cute interests. **Adjustable:** categories, number and arrangement. **Optional:** labels, but only if the visual relationship survives without extensive explanation. **Boundary:** causal scenes show events; this recipe shows shape mapping. A mascot wearing niche clothing is neither.

### Antique/vintage treasure hunters

Fit: looking closely changes what the finder sees. Hypothesis: an apparent ordinary mark revealing an object rewards the hunter's eye. Nonfit: generic retro patterns with no discovery behavior.

- **C1 — Look again.** Three simple scratches become, on closer inspection, silhouettes of a chair profile, a key and a small studio vase, using their existing line geometry rather than placing objects behind scratches. Minimal “Look again” copy. The visual recognition enacts noticing overlooked possibility. Risk: if transformations are not immediately plausible, it becomes a puzzle requiring explanation.
- **C2 — The route becomes the find.** Three small detour paths resolve into outlines of a record, a lamp and an odd sculptural vessel, with start/finish dots retained as route cues. Discovery and journey share the same line. Risk: recognizability and density; imagery must look like routes and objects, not arbitrary squiggles.

### Remote-working parents

Fit: paid-work structures and parenting structures occupy the same space. Hypothesis: a visual fusion of those structures produces recognition. Nonfit: generic productivity symbolism or implying all parents can smoothly multitask.

- **C3 — Calendar construction.** Rectangular calendar blocks become toy building blocks across three tiny transformations, retaining day-grid cues in each. A school-pickup block forms a bridge between work blocks. The visual mapping expresses building a day around both roles. Risk: needs strong graphic simplification; it must not resemble proprietary game blocks.
- **C4 — Two kinds of tabs.** Browser-tab outlines transition into school-folder tabs, then overlap in a compact three-part sequence. Short labels alternate work deadline and school task. The shared geometry turns mental context switching into a visible double category. Risk: without restrained labels it may read as office stationery rather than parenting.

**Test outcome:** PARTIAL. The recipe generates candidates, but all four depend on successful visual execution; prose does not establish visual legibility. C1/C2 are the more speculative applications and should not be promoted merely because the explanation is clever. **REVISE the current family:** shape mapping must be separately findable, with its shape-legibility condition explicit. Do not solve a weak sketch by inventing additional source evidence.

## What this exercise establishes

- Understanding: generally good at individual-card level, uneven at family level.
- Identity assessment: possible using external identity context, but the current library needs concise applicability conditions and failure cases to make that assessment repeatable.
- Creation: twelve candidate sketches were generated across six pairings. Delayed reversal and borrowed formats are easier to operationalize; shape mapping needs actual visual testing.
- The reviewer supplied missing abstraction and constraints during the exercise. That is the reason for REVISE; successful improvisation by a capable agent is not evidence that the existing document already contains a complete handoff.
- No concept is approved for production, globally unique, commercially validated, or an independent external corroboration of its source recipe. No new artwork was rendered.

## Minimum revision and shared-library recommendation

Keep the eleven family names as provisional navigation where useful; do not force the library to remain exactly eleven. Before revising all sixty records, improve one actionable recipe from each of the three tested families using this small card:

1. Stable ID/version and plain-language operation.
2. Two to four required conditions; adjustable/optional elements.
3. Fits when / fails when; closest neighboring recipe.
4. Source links and source-specific copying boundary.
5. One illustrative application clearly separated from observed source evidence.

Recommended shared location: `docs/creative/trace/mechanisms/`, with an index linking families → stable recipe files → platform-specific source records. Do not duplicate commercial evidence into universal mechanism claims. Future Etsy, Amazon or DTC decodes can link to an existing recipe or propose a new one after individual decoding; retain revision history if families split or merge.

This review is saved outside the Etsy pilot folder. It does not itself relocate the library or edit historical cards.
