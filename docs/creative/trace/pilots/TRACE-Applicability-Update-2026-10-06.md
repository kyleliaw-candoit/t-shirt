# TRACE — approved applicability update, October 6, 2026

> Superseded for prospective work by [TRACE v0.4](../../../company/TRACE-Bestseller-Creative-Intelligence-Methodology.md), approved later in the October 6 discussion. The current standard removes target-community applicability and unknown origin as TRACE analysis/acceptance filters. This note is retained as the earlier decision record; its origin checks and supported-target-input requirements are not current TRACE gates.

Status: founder-approved direction recorded during the October 6 discussion. Prospective addendum; not a rerun, retrospective validation, or rewrite of frozen TRACE v0.3.

## Decision

Do not require a candidate recipe to succeed in every community of the pilot panel before it can inform original concept exploration in a suitable niche.

Evaluate and document the conditions a mechanism needs, then assess whether independently supported target-community truths meet those conditions. A promising fit in one community may proceed to concept development and consideration for a bounded demand test under the existing brand, originality and spending gates. This decision does not authorize spending or production.

Separate:
- **Candidate recipe and niche fit:** a hypothesized mechanism with explicit ingredients, applicability conditions, supported community inputs and an original concept.
- **Broader reuse evidence:** successful transfer across additional unrelated truths or communities strengthens the reuse claim. Lack of universal fit is not itself disqualification. No replacement numeric validation threshold is adopted by this note.
- **Customer demand:** assessed separately through behavioral testing; creative transfer alone does not establish sales potential.

## Correction to the spoken explanation

The October 3 [method debrief](./TRACE-PILOT-002-Method-Debrief-2026-10-03.md) already states that TRACE required at least three unrelated truth instantiations, not success in every arbitrary community. The pilot effectively required success on all three selected inputs because it used one narrow truth per community. The October 6 spoken explanation overstated the general rule as an all-three-community requirement.

The change approved today is to remove that pilot outcome as a blanket barrier to exploratory use where a mechanism plausibly fits, while keeping broader validation claims separate.

## Preserved findings and safeguards

Ten source cards yielded two recipe candidates and eight grammar-only observations. The six original transfer attempts yielded five rejected attempts and one conditional fit. Neither recipe earned validation. The follow-up stopped for insufficient corroborated inputs before new concept generation.

Keep the original HOLD labels and outcomes as historical results under the frozen protocol. Prospectively, both mechanisms remain unvalidated candidates available for applicability-led exploration; this is not promotion to official validated recipes.

Keep source-origin checks, copying and transfer prohibitions, evidence standards, and ingredient definitions. Do not fabricate community truths, silently broaden a mechanism to rescue an attempt, or conceal failures. State applicability conditions before generating new concepts and record all attempted outcomes. A revised full methodology, future panel or new experiment is not created by this note.

## Next discussion

These are discussion categories, not a new counted failure breakdown.

Read the underlying records before assigning candidate counts to those causes. The same candidate can encounter multiple gates; do not sum overlapping reasons as separate failed candidates.
