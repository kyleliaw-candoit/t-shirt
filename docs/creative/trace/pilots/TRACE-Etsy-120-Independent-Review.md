## Granularity, essential conditions and single-source recipes

The catalog is useful as an operational library after these corrections, but **not a taxonomy of 68 equally broad mechanisms**. TRM-005 is a deliberately broad wordplay operation; its variants are now explicit. Before using it in a brief, name the original reading, the exact phonetic/segmentation move and the resolving cue. Its22 observed pairs do not establish that it is twice as effective as004 or better than a one-source visual operation. Further subdivision is unnecessary for this documentation checkpoint; compare a specific variant’s fit when selecting a future brief.

Highest-score review:004 needs two natural readings and a resolving cue;005 needs recoverable wordplay and a second meaning;019 needs visible epic agency against an ordinary/tiny subject, not costume alone;008 needs an actual changed beneficiary;018 needs text/image tension. These conditions are now usable. The5-point018 and008 and7-point019 remain candidates, not sales recommendations. The4-point group includes007,012,038,040,047 and051; their distinct requirements are figurative creature naming, disproportionate stakes, threat-to-tenderness, late restriction, visible bodily causality and capability-to-task use, respectively.

Keep019/048 overlapping: epic agency can coexist with a martial rally, but the rally additionally requires mobilization toward a mundane collective task. Keep003/037 overlapping: real shared geometry and makeshift magical aspiration are separately visible in117. Keep038/052 separately available: threat displaced by a gentle gesture is not the same instruction as an imposing figure absorbed in a quiet pastime. Keep006/028 separate: changing an acronym’s expansion differs from redefining a reputation, despite both borrowing dictionary conventions. Keep010 specific: its word split creates a route choice; not every instance of005 does that. These boundaries guide creation without forcing exclusive classification.

After merging030 and splitting071, **46 recipes have one source expression**. This is not a failure or proof of low adoption under current TRACE. Additional seller appearances repeat that expression; they do not turn one source into multiple independent creative examples. All remain CANDIDATE; no corroboration maturity upgrade was made.

Other unchanged cards’ conditions and cited descriptions supply a sufficiently clear candidate operation; their fit statements are guidance, not tested audience findings.

Some existing illustrative applications are rough prompts rather than successful demonstrations (notably005’s unspecified tool pun and010’s unspecified word split). ## Score and commercial-evidence assessment

Independent recomputation confirmed the original arithmetic and the corrected mappings. The rule remains **one point per distinct expression plus one per additional resolved seller offering that expression or a cosmetic variant**. Same-seller garment variants and same-maker distribution earn no extra points. Pair uniqueness uses source-expression ID and resolved storefront identity; corporate independence is not established. Distinct expressions from the same seller still earn separate design points.

The retained corpus is **113 non-tentative source interpretations +55 additional appearances =168 pairs**. The new60 contribute60+34=94, comprising29 resolved external offers and five ordered-scan recurrences. Legacy sources contribute53+21=74, including eight recurrence pairs from the continuation. Reviews and badges carry no numerical weight. Every recipe uses its source-supported pair set; families use unions, not sums. Families and recipes may overlap, so neither set of totals should be added to estimate corpus size.

Reviewed all additional-pair explanations for expression versus mechanism conflation. Examples with different treatment (octopus/drums42; dachshund/festival pun37; frog/goose56) retain the same specific subject/action or phrase relationship under the run’s close-cosmetic-variant rule. This is weaker than matching original artwork and is not independent creative corroboration. Search snippets alone, unresolved identities, different expressions and same-maker offers remain uncredited. The evidence is auditable as recorded explanations/URLs, not as archived product-page proof.

## Non-blocking evidence limitations

- **A7–A8:** admitted records and detail decisions survive; full card rosters do not. A9–A20 and B1 retain771 positions. Cannot reconstruct all early exclusions, verify complete traversal or estimate unbiased yield. A current search would not recreate the historical order. No missing roster was fabricated.
- **Unarchived screenshots:** cannot independently verify exact graphics, badge state, image legibility or all visual deduplication decisions at scan time. The documentation review accepts bounded operator records under current partial-evidence rules; it does not erase this gap.
- **Uneven follow-up:** all60 have a recorded bounded attempt, but only29 have a resolved external credit. Availability and one-product selection differ; legacy coverage also differs. Uncredited coverage remains unknown, never zero adoption. “Up to three” candidate offers did not mean three product verifications.
- **Commercial inference:** Bestseller is recorded at listing level; reviews may pool garments, colors and phrases. External offers establish recorded availability/recurrence, not sales, demand causality, independent ownership, original authorship, popularity rank or permission to copy. The ranked table orders observed scores only.

These are non-blocking because the output is an explicitly limited candidate library, current TRACE permits partial records, and this PR makes no production or representative-market claim. They would block stronger claims of independently verified visuals, complete sampling or proven commercial winners.
