## Individual evidence and follow-up

All new designs were inspected on Etsy. Source listing ID, seller, position, visible design, interpretation, ingredients and caveats are retained in each card; IDs are the stable source references. Live screenshots informed inspection, but this deliverable does not contain a screenshot archive.

US Google Shopping was attempted for each of the 55, with a cap of three representative organic offers per query. **38 queries yielded readable organic offer captures.** The other **17** received targeted web fallback: 16 have a product/catalog reference or related variant, and one adult novelty design remains without a useful independent outside source. A fallback may be only a related expression or mechanism; it is not automatically an exact match.

Eight earlier Shopping captures had no readable offer extraction. The final nine-query batch also lacked readable offers; subsequent inspection showed Google's unusual-traffic/CAPTCHA block. The exact onset within that batch was not established. Browser access to Google stopped and the block was reported; no CAPTCHA or bypass was attempted. Empty captures do not establish that offers are absent.

The cards record actual queries, merchant labels, distinctions between exact expression and related mechanism, and representative product URLs where obtained. Shopping-only rows have no independently inspected merchant destination. Merchant names may be unresolved, marketplace offers may syndicate Etsy listings, and duplicate garments/sizes are not independent sellers. Outside artwork was not uniformly inspected. These observations describe distribution/recurrence, not independent invention.

Review/rating corrections matter: Zazzle's large totals included similar products; Etsy multi-design listings pooled evidence; eBay seller feedback is separate from the one sold count on a frog/goose offer; Target's cat variant had one rating; a live viewer counter is not demand. No pooled totals were promoted to source-design sales evidence. Source-specific limitations remain in the cards.

## Findings and next use

The method can extract visual-only mechanisms: the dandelion cryptid, dog stealing a skeleton's arm, pasta-shaped dogs and octopus drummer all depend on a visible relationship rather than merely a graphic style. Conversely, a strong graphic or cause statement may sell without yielding a separate reusable transformation under this scope.

Simple mechanisms require the most careful boundary work. Age-as-vintage, collection justification and direct advocacy can be close to ordinary identity statements; keeping an explicit alternative explanation is more useful than manufacturing certainty.

Recommended workflow refinements for the next scan: keep card-level provisional skips separate from detail-confirmed exclusions; open uncertain generic-title cases; complete and record the first-five checkpoint on time; capture badge location and evidence scope explicitly; store direct merchant references as they are visited. This report proposes those refinements without silently changing the governing methodology.

Recommended next step after reviewing this run: choose three to five candidate recipes for a small original-concept exercise, then assess the resulting concepts in the existing downstream process. Use fresh audience truths and scenes, not source-word substitutions or close visual derivatives. No further broad scan, Amazon run, production design or behavioral validation has been undertaken here.
