## Individual concept lineage and rejection records

### TRACE-P002-XFR-001 — prestigious office, ordinary care task

- Recipe/truth/grammar: CR-001 v0.1 + TRACE-P002-NT-001 (R010) + TRACE-P002-AGR-001.
- Supporting lineage: EF-002 / SDC-002 only; its entire prohibited-elements list is part of this handoff.
- Attempted slots: prestigious work frame → domestic care task as modest reward → parent as willing participant → affectionate status reversal.
- Proposed wording/imagery: working label “Executive suite”; a compact work nameplate beside an open lunchbox and a child's care schedule. Text description only.
- Intended identity/payoff/role: recognition of work/care role collision; possible attention and wearable self-recognition.
- Finding: the locked truth describes repeated responsibility transitions. It does not establish that extra care work is a low-status reward the participant willingly seeks. Without that preference the concept becomes a situational joke, not this recipe. Treating overload as pleasure would misrepresent the truth.
- Shirt-fit hypothesis: one nameplate and lunchbox may read quickly, but the schedule risks fine-detail clutter and the low-status framing may alienate the wearer.
- Required evidence: independent evidence of the particular preferred reward and participant response; actual comprehension and wearability evidence. None supplied here. REJECT without replacing the truth.

### TRACE-P002-XFR-002 — award presentation for an uncertain outcome

- Recipe/truth/grammar: CR-001 v0.1 + TRACE-P002-NT-002 (R021) + TRACE-P002-AGR-006.
- Supporting lineage: EF-002 / SDC-002 only; all source prohibitions retained.
- Attempted slots: award ceremony → flawed finished vessel as reward → maker presenting it → affectionate reversal.
- Proposed wording/imagery: working label “The grand result”; one maker's hands presenting an irregular vessel on a small pedestal.
- Intended identity/payoff/role: recognition of kiln uncertainty; intended wry amusement and attention.
- Finding: the locked truth supports risk and anticipation, not preferring defects. An irregular form could be deliberate rather than a kiln failure, losing even the intended premise. Neither I3 nor I4 is established. A proposed joke about tolerating disappointment is a different mechanism from willingly choosing the modest reward.
- Shirt-fit hypothesis: one vessel is compact; its intended flaw and the emotional distinction may not read at feed size.
- Required evidence: truth-grounded preference, legible outcome cue and wearer reaction. No evidence added to rescue the slot. REJECT.

### TRACE-P002-XFR-003 — ceremonial discovery of an ordinary-looking fragment

- Recipe/truth/grammar: CR-001 v0.1 + TRACE-P002-NT-003 (R027) + TRACE-P002-AGR-006.
- Supporting lineage: EF-002 / SDC-002 only; source wording, actors, vehicle, slogan syntax and commercial appearance prohibited.

- Proposed wording/imagery: working label “The reveal”; a plain rectangular cutaway showing a small original-floor patch beneath a lifted later covering, with a disproportionately formal presentation cushion beside it. No actual artwork.
- Intended identity/payoff/role: pride in recognizing historic fabric that may look unimpressive to an outsider; affectionate self-recognition, with wearability as the intended role.
