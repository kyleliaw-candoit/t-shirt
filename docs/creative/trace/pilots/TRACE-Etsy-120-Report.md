## Observed adoption and coverage

**Score = distinct designs + additional seller appearances = distinct design–seller pairs.** The reconciled evidence contains **168 unique credited pairs: 113 source designs + 55 additional seller appearances**. This includes **94 pairs for the new60: 60 designs +34 additional appearances**. Storefront identities are resolved for scoring; independent ownership, authorship, purchase volume and whether copied offers are themselves bestsellers are not established.

Every new design received one focused follow-up query, up to three candidate organic offers and one selected product-page attempt. Google Shopping's browser route encountered a safeguard, so web/product retrieval was labeled as a fallback. No attempt was made to bypass it. **29/60** designs earned a resolved external-offer credit; the other31 have unknown external coverage, not evidence of zero adoption. Five additional new-design pairs came from ordered Etsy recurrence. Historical product observations were reconciled conservatively: same-maker distribution, unresolved sellers, incomplete graphics and different expressions receive no additional point. Eight further legacy-design pairs were observed during this continuation's ordered scan.

The cap makes effort bounded, not coverage equal. Some selected pages were unavailable, category pages, original sources or wrong matches. Search-result candidates alone receive no points. Only one selected product attempt per query was used, even where up to three candidates were recorded; this follows the pilot's bounded policy, not an exhaustive merchant census. Do not interpret a low observed score as low market adoption. Earlier source-image observations were reused for legacy mapping, not represented as newly re-inspected artwork.
