# TRACE-PILOT-002 — method debrief, October 3, 2026

Status: completed operator debrief. Governing TRACE v0.3 remains unchanged. This is not an independent review.

## What the run established

The frozen scan produced ten eligible DTC families, two source-level recipe candidates and eight grammar-only observations. Amazon contributed no selected families and Etsy no eligible families; this is a DTC-only sample, not cross-platform validation. Native ranks do not isolate design effects or show rank persistence.

The [six transfer diagnostics](./TRACE-PILOT-002-Transfer-Diagnostics-and-Findings.md) produced five rejected attempts and one conditional structural fit. Neither abstract recipe demonstrated the three unrelated truth instantiations required for validity. Both remain HOLD, with no maturity assignment, behavioral evidence or production-ready concept.

The source screening, preserved failures, explicit copying prohibitions, canonical identity joins and separation of grammar from semantic recipes were useful controls. The separate panel and ingredient commits made the order reviewable.

## What we should correct in our interpretation

**Failure on this panel is not evidence that a recipe cannot transfer.** Each identity contributed only one narrow research excerpt. R010 supplied responsibility collision; R021 supplied delayed failure risk; R027 supplied discovery reward. We effectively required success on all three particular inputs. TRACE requires at least three unrelated truth instantiations; it does not assert that every valid recipe must fit every community or arbitrary truth. We must still test every frozen panel identity and retain failures.

A single seed per cell confounds recipe limitations, input suitability and the operator's creative execution. The result is an unsuccessful demonstration under that budget, not an estimate of a recipe's success rate. The one conditional fit is not a passed shirt or audience preference.

The two recipe hypotheses also require different inputs. CR-001 needs willing valuation of a modest reward; accounts of involuntary overload or defects cannot supply that. CR-002 needs a coherent activity expression plus a lexical agent link; a metaphor or personified object alone cannot supply that. Adding a smile or declaring a preference would fabricate the missing premise.

The source support matrix contains interpretive ingredients: willingness and payoff in CR-001, dual-reading resolution in CR-002. We should preserve those as hypotheses rather than present them as measured buyer psychology. One source each gives no independent corroboration; repeated observation of that source in P001 adds none.

The prior panel was exposed, and its truths were canonical analyst summaries rather than newly verified participant statements. A timestamped lock fixes procedural ordering for P002, not blinding or evidence depth.

## Decision

Retain both v0.1 definitions, all six outcomes and all source decisions. Do not broaden a recipe to rescue a failed slot. Do not rewrite the frozen method as v0.4 merely because two candidates did not pass.

Run a separate, prospective **input-adequacy follow-up** under [P002-F01](./TRACE-PILOT-002-F01-Evidence-Followup-Preregistration.md). It keeps R010/R021/R027 and looks for primary evidence of positive preferences and activity language within a fixed public-search budget. This is targeted exploratory evidence collection informed by the failed test, not independent confirmation.

Separate three judgments:
1. Is the community claim actually supported?
2. Does the frozen recipe fit that claim?
3. Does the resulting expression work as a shirt?

Input adequacy must be decided and committed before new concept generation. If the evidence budget does not support three identities for a recipe, record insufficiency and stop that recipe's follow-up. Do not expand the budget automatically or count absence of retrieved evidence as absence of the behavior.

## What is worth reviewing

The decision-bearing change is the separation of inadequate inputs from failed creative transfer. Review that interpretation and the follow-up's finite search budget. The original HOLD decisions remain correct; the earlier shorthand “neither recipe passed” must be read with its panel and one-attempt limitations.

A useful follow-up can end with an evidence gap. Its success criterion is a defensible decision about whether another transfer test is warranted, not producing three passing concepts.

## Business implication

The eight grammar observations can inform original composition work subject to the normal brand and design gates. No TRACE recipe is ready to be treated as a repeatable design engine. More broad bestseller screening is not the immediate remedy for missing target-community evidence, and no advertising or production expenditure is warranted by this run alone.
