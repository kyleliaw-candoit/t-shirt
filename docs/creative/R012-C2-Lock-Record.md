# R012 Concept C2 — Found Not New — Booth 42

**Creative Territory:** C — The Find Has a Story

**Core recognition:** the object's story includes the hunt context itself; the antique-mall booth ticket makes the design more specific to people who browse, hunt, and find in antique malls.

## Founder-approved visual decisions

- Unisex first-MVV execution on the controlled warm off-white / ivory garment.
- Center-chest composition; do not revert to the earlier off-center left-chest placement.
- Overall graphic remains medium-small with generous negative space so the two-object grouping feels balanced rather than oversized.
- Aged kraft/tan antique dealer price tag remains the hero object.
- Copy: `Found not new. That’s the point.`
- Expressive hand-lettering remains integrated into the tag, with a small faded-rust underline accent beneath the final line.
- Moss-green `BOOTH 42` antique-mall ticket is the secondary cue.
- Booth ticket is slightly angled and partially overlaps / tucks against the lower-right side of the tag, following the founder-approved Option C relationship.
- Ticket should feel worn and plausible, with classic notched / perforated-edge artifact character rather than a modern retail sticker.
- Ticket remains subordinate to the tag: it strengthens antique-hunting specificity without becoming a second headline.
- Dark cord/string and reinforced eyelet remain part of the tag character.
- Palette: dark olive/charcoal, warm tan/brown, moss green, and a small faded-rust accent.
- Patina belongs to the depicted objects; avoid generic grunge.
- Front print only for the controlled first-MVV execution.
