Audience: repeat antique and vintage treasure hunters, including casual flea-market shoppers, collectors, decorators and odd-object enthusiasts. Recognizability means familiarity within this audience, not general-public comprehension.

Source: [complete material bank](../Researches/R012-audience-evidence/Material-Bank.md). Method: [Creative Director methodology §5.6](../company/Creative-Director-Methodology.md#56-required-for-every-identity--rank-material-recognizability-before-concepts).

## Evidence dependence and audience boundaries

E007/E008 share a writer/page; E009/E010 share a writer/page; E011/E012/E019 share a regret-prompted discussion. These are not independent samples. E002 offers a separate regret source, but prompted regret threads still cannot establish population frequency. E005's multiple voices occur on one page. Technical accuracy in E013–E018 does not establish community recognition. Preserve the source bank's specific venue, subgroup and dating limitations.

Moderate confidence expresses a plausible cross-category recognition hypothesis, not a representative estimate. The sample is purposive, English-language, often old, and biased toward specialists/public writers. Casual-noncollector and dedicated vintage-shop/antique-mall coverage remains limited. Specialist facts may rank much higher within their own subgroups.

## Creative priority

Start with three distinct material groups: missed-find regret (E002/E011), household objects evoking family memories (E005), and transport constraints (E007/E012). Related entries support variations; they do not automatically create additional territories. Retain concrete details and distinguish source reports from invented scenarios.

Recognition priority is separate from mechanism frequency, humor quality, wearability and purchase interest. The Founder accepted this order for exploration, not production. The Founder subsequently prioritized humor for this test. [Six initial concepts](R012-Initial-Concepts-2026-10-19.md) therefore retain missed-find regret and transport constraints but substitute E004 (collection completion versus continued desire) for nostalgia-led E005. This documented creative-priority exception does not alter estimated recognizability; E005 remains available for later warm/sincere work. Revisit this ranking when targeted corroboration or audience evidence changes the judgment.
