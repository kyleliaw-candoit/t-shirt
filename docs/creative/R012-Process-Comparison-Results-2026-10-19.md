# R012 — Creative-process comparison: results and judgment calibration

2026-10-19 · All 24 Founder ratings received · Exploratory results, not an official methodology change

## Main finding

Our judgments are only partly aligned. Several large disagreements concern whether the shirt conveys its underlying situation and audience without explanation. The Creative Director often gave more credit to verbal construction and inferred context than the Founder did. That is an interpretation of the rating comments, not proof that one rater is objectively superior.

The three workflows have material-dependent results. Explicit analysis plus recipes is a promising working default to investigate, with free drafting retained as a meaningful alternative. This trial does not establish a reliable winner or authorize an official methodology change.

## Provisional calibration to test, not adopted methodology

1. **Judge what is present on the shirt.** State what a new viewer can infer from the exact words and specified visual; do not supply the episode from background knowledge. In particular, an object being called “vintage” in an internal direction does not guarantee the rendered object communicates that.
2. **Require a legible situation before crediting cleverness.** Identify whether the intended behavior is recoverable without an explanation. Do not require every chronological detail to be stated literally; enough information must support the intended turn.
3. **Evaluate audience relevance explicitly.** Test whether the wording and image together establish the intended find/collector context. A sale tag alone may be insufficient; a setting cue can help when it is doing real explanatory work. The Founder proposed a yard-sale/flea-market cue for N-B.
4. **Value an understandable final reveal.** T-D and T-E show that simple setups and clear reversals can outperform a more elaborate analogy. This is a lesson from these ratings, not a universal formula.
5. **Separate humor, emotional appeal and shirt desirability.** N-D can be an excellent warm concept without being a strong joke. Record these dimensions separately in future reviews; do not penalize or promote it under the wrong objective.
6. **Keep reasons for disagreement.** Original ratings stay intact. Later calibration should improve prediction and selection, not simply imitate Founder numbers or assume Founder taste represents customers.

Working recommendation: use explicit material analysis as a provisional starting discipline, explore both recipe-based and free alternatives, and compare them under stronger clarity and audience-relevance checks. Do not require recipes to win or discard them: T-D and N-A use TRM-002, while T-E came from free drafting. All three methods can still fail when the material-to-audience connection is weak.

## Limits and next decision

One author, shared context, prior recipe knowledge, four selected material groups and two finalists per method per material cannot establish repeatable superiority. Founder familiarity with the source material and text-only visual descriptions affect interpretation. No customers or rendered shirt visuals were tested. Originality checks remain downstream.

Review the calibration lessons and working process recommendation before any official Creative Director methodology change. This document records the comparison; it does not enact that change. PR remains unmerged.
