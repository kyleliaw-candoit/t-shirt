## Decision-ready findings

- A qualifying one-class federal application currently starts at **$350**, not the historical $250 TEAS Plus fee. A Section 1(b) intent-to-use application ordinarily needs another **$150/class** for its later allegation of use. See the fee package below.
- Registration is **not legally mandatory merely to begin selling**, but deferral is not name clearance. An application is not a registration. [USPTO registration guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/why-register-your-trademark)
- Artwork can remain unbranded. Genuine source-identifying labels, attached hangtags, packaging or qualifying real point-of-sale webpages are possible goods specimens. A mock-up/private preview or invented transaction is not evidence of actual use. [USPTO specimens](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/laws/specimen-refusal-and-how-overcome-refusal)
- Taukay LLC doing business under a different name presents a Texas SOS assumed-name question, not an automatic need for a second LLC. The state certificate is **$25**; LLCs no longer also file at county level. [Texas SOS Form 503 instructions](https://www.sos.texas.gov/corp/instructions/503.shtml)
- A Texas sales-tax permit has **no application fee**, but activates return/record obligations even in zero-activity periods. Existing permit status must be checked before creating a duplicate. [Comptroller permit FAQ](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/sales/faq/permit.php)
- Current FinCEN guidance exempts **U.S.-created entities** from BOI reporting. Do not create a stale domestic-LLC BOI task. [FinCEN BOI](https://www.fincen.gov/boi)
- Frisco's older home-occupation material must be read alongside newer state law. A qualifying no-impact home-based business cannot be required by a municipality to obtain an operating permit/license. Actual premises and activity facts are unresolved. ## A. Research Analyst: federal trademark facts

### A1. Mark, ownership and representation

Candidate for review: **STILL A FIND**, standard characters, no logo/color claim. Standard-character registration concerns the wording without restriction to a particular font, size or color; it is not ownership of the phrase for all purposes. The approved tagline and individual shirt graphics are outside this proposed mark. [USPTO drawing guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/mark-drawings-trademarks)

Owner must be the party that owns the mark (Section 1(a)) or possesses the bona fide intention to use it (Section 1(b)) on the application date. Filing in the wrong party's name can be an incurable defect, unlike a correctable naming error. Do not select Kyle personally, Taukay LLC or a nonexistent “Still a Find LLC” merely for convenience. [TMEP §1201.02(b)](https://tmep.uspto.gov/RDMS/TMEP/print?href=TMEP-1200d1e112.html&version=current)

A U.S.-domiciled applicant is not required to hire counsel; a foreign-domiciled applicant needs a U.S.-licensed attorney. For an entity, domicile is its actual headquarters where senior executives/officers normally direct and control its activities, not automatically its formation state, registered agent or mail service. Kyle's Frisco location alone does not finish this factual check. [Attorney requirement](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/do-i-need-attorney), [USPTO domicile rule](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/laws/trademark-rule-requires-foreign-applicants-and-registrants-have-us)

Actual filing route: Kyle, if legally authorized to bind the verified owner and eligible to self-file, or approved U.S. counsel. USPTO accounts belong to verified individuals, cannot be shared, and signatures must be personally made by the signatory. Non-attorney filing businesses do not get an owner role merely because a client hires them. This packet does not promise autonomous filing by an assistant. [USPTO user-role rules](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply/trademark-user-roles)

Privacy checkpoint before any filing: the application becomes public. An accurate separate mailing address is public, while the domicile address can be entered in its dedicated non-public field. Putting the home address in other public fields or attachments can expose it. Never substitute a fake domicile or commit private address/identity records to GitHub. [USPTO personal-information guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply/faqs-personal-information-trademark-records)

### A2. Factual basis, not a cost-driven choice

| Basis | Facts required | Current project evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Section 1(a), use in commerce | Actual qualifying use with every claimed good/service as of filing; truthful first-use-anywhere and first-use-in-commerce dates; genuine specimen per class; signed verification | Not established. A planned Shopify store, domain reservation, art mock-up or test plan is insufficient by itself. |
| Section 1(b), intent to use | Bona fide intent to use the mark in commerce at filing; truthful declaration; later acceptable allegation of actual use before registration | Possible review path for a prelaunch project, not a factual determination or selected filing basis. Preserve ordinary dated business planning evidence. |

For goods, the mark must identify the goods through permitted use and the goods must actually be sold or transported in commerce. For services, the services must actually be rendered. Do not invent first-use dates, manufacture token evidence, or treat an internal test order as automatically qualifying. [USPTO basis requirements](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply/basis)

### A3. Class 025 is not interchangeable with Class 035

- **025:** clothing goods. Review the narrow candidate identification “T-shirts” in the live Trademark ID Manual when preparing the actual application. Add other goods only if the selected owner actually uses or has a genuine qualifying intent to use the mark for them. The source supports clothing as one class; final ID Manual selection remains a pre-filing check. [USPTO goods/services](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/goods-and-services), [USPTO fee example](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/trademark-fee-information)
- **035:** retail-store services, not the T-shirt goods themselves. A possible separate review candidate is online retail store services featuring clothing. USPTO cautions that “sales” of one's own goods alone is not a registrable service identification. Determine the real service, source-identifying use and protection objective rather than buying this class automatically because Shopify is involved. [USPTO ID Manual guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/guides-and-manuals/guidance-users)
- Related goods/services may matter across classes. A class number is an administrative category, not a complete conflict boundary. Do not read a 025-only filing as clearing similar retail marks, or a 035 filing as a substitute for goods protection. [USPTO coordinated classes](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/using-coordinated-classes-your-federal-trademark-search)

### A6. Deadlines to record only after a real filing/event

- Most examination office actions have a three-month response period, with a paid three-month extension available; the actual notice controls and exceptions exist. [USPTO response periods](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply/response-time-period)
- After a Notice of Allowance, an ITU applicant has six months to file a statement of use or extension. Up to five six-month extensions are possible, with use/SOU required within three years of the NOA. These periods run from NOA issuance, not from when an extension is filed. [USPTO ITU forms](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply/intent-use-itu-forms)
- After registration: Section 8 during years 5–6; combined Sections 8/9 during years 9–10 and each subsequent ten-year cycle. Limited grace periods have extra fees; do not plan to depend on them. [USPTO maintenance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/maintain/keeping-your-registration-alive)

## B. Operations fact-check hat: Texas and federal business administration

### B1. Assumed name, not a new entity

An LLC regularly conducting Texas business under a name different from its legal name must file an assumed-name certificate with Texas SOS. The working review candidate is Taukay LLC using Still a Find, contingent on confirming exact legal entity and any existing filing. A certificate can last up to ten years; renewal uses a new certificate. LLC county-level filing was eliminated effective September 1, 2019. The certificate does not grant exclusive name rights. [Form 503 instructions](https://www.sos.texas.gov/corp/instructions/503.shtml), [SOS name FAQ](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/namefilingsfaqs.shtml)

Current government fee: **$25**. SOSPortal credit-card processing adds **$0.25 + 2.25% of total fees**; ACH is listed as another payment method. Confirm the exact final total and payment terms before requesting approval. No expedited service assumed. Gather privately: exact entity name/type, jurisdiction, SOS record/file reference, address, duration, counties of use and authorized signer. Do not guess a single county from “Frisco.” [Form 503 payment instructions](https://www.sos.texas.gov/corp/instructions/503.shtml)

### B2. Sales/use tax and direct-store operations

A Texas business selling taxable tangible goods generally needs a sales/use tax permit. The permit is **$0**, although a security bond can be required in some circumstances. Existing ownership/location/outlet status must be checked; a new brand does not by itself prove a new permit is needed. Permit holders must report even periods without taxable sales/purchases. [Comptroller permit FAQ](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/sales/faq/permit.php)

Texas sellers using a marketplace still generally need a permit and timely returns even when the marketplace collects tax. Do not infer that a direct Shopify storefront has the same collection arrangement as a qualifying marketplace. Confirm actual platform responsibilities, fulfillment locations, ship-to destinations, sourcing and any resale-certificate workflow before configuring tax settings. [Comptroller marketplace guidance](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/sales/marketplace-providers-sellers.php), [Online orders](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/publications/94-171.php)

Texas state sales tax is 6.25%, plus applicable local taxes up to 2%, maximum 8.25%; this is not an instruction to hard-code 8.25% on every order. Filing frequency comes from the account assignment. Monthly returns ordinarily fall on the following month's 20th; quarterly returns on April 20, July 20, October 20 and January 20; annual returns on January 20, subject to official date adjustments. [Comptroller sales tax](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/sales/)

### B3. Existing LLC franchise/PIR obligations

For 2026 and 2027 reports, the no-tax-due threshold is **$2,650,000 annualized total revenue**. Entities at/below it no longer file the old No Tax Due Report, but the information-report obligation remains. An LLC ordinarily files a **Public Information Report**, not an OIR. Normal annual deadline is May 15, adjusted to the next business day for weekends/holidays. [2026 official forms/instructions](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/forms/2026-franchise.php), [PIR entity types](https://comptroller.texas.gov/help/franchise/information-report.php), [Franchise tax dates/thresholds](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/)

Taukay's formation date, relevant report years, exemptions, combined-group position, annualized revenue and account standing have not been checked. These are existing-entity obligations, not automatically reset by a brand launch. Do not assert tax is zero or that a filing was missed. Privately verify current status and prior returns first; route substantive accounting/tax treatment to Finance or a qualified tax professional.

### B4. Federal BOI and EIN

Current FinCEN guidance says all U.S.-created entities and their beneficial owners are exempt from BOI reporting under the revised rule. If Taukay was formed in a U.S. state, do not initiate a BOI filing solely because it is an LLC. A Texas “foreign LLC” formed in another U.S. state is different from an entity formed under foreign-country law; confirm actual formation jurisdiction. Foreign-country entities registered in the U.S. can have different requirements. [FinCEN current BOI guidance](https://www.fincen.gov/boi)

Do not obtain another EIN merely for a brand change. IRS guidance says a business name/address change alone does not require a new EIN, and a business entity should generally have one EIN. Existing EIN, legal tax identity, tax classification and bank/payout readiness remain private verification tasks, not public-document fields. [IRS EIN guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/employer-identification-number)

### B5. Frisco/local requirements: fact-dependent, no blanket clearance

Texas has no general statewide business license, but activity-specific licenses and tax requirements still exist. [Governor's Business Permit Office](https://gov.texas.gov/business/page/business-permits-office)

Texas Local Government Code §229.902, effective June 12, 2025, restricts municipal regulation of qualifying no-impact home businesses. Relevant conditions include occupancy compliance, no generated on-street parking/substantial traffic increase, no activities visible from a street and no substantial noise increase/noise violation. Municipal operating permits/licenses cannot be required for that qualifying category. Other lawful requirements and HOA/private agreements remain. [Current statute](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/LG/htm/LG.229.htm), [Enactment history](https://capitol.texas.gov/BillLookup/History.aspx?Bill=HB2464&LegSess=89R)

Frisco's current FAQ still directs home-based businesses to home-occupation/zoning rules, and its linked ordinance dates to 2013. Do not apply that document without the later state-law qualification. Frisco also offers address-specific zoning research and optional $50 zoning-verification letters; a letter is not established as required for this online POD operation. [Frisco FAQ](https://www.friscotexas.gov/m/faq), [Ordinance index](https://www.friscotexas.gov/481/Most-Requested-Ordinances), [Zoning information](https://www.friscotexas.gov/2025/Zoning-Verification-Event-Letters)

Unresolved privately: actual city limits/jurisdiction and premises; home-office only versus production/storage; customer pickup/visits; employees; signage; deliveries/traffic/noise; lease/HOA limits. Commercial premises, pop-ups/events, local manufacturing or a later change in operating footprint need a separate applicable-permit check. No local filing fee is added to the launch budget on these unknown facts.
