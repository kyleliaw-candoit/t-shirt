## 2. Strategy Advisor hat

### Recommendation: preserve #110 and resolve Gate 1 before implementation

The launch packet is useful, economical preparation and correctly avoids duplicate accounts, duplicate preview work and premature filing. It is **not a complete Store Build Packet**. Continue the existing two-fixture experiment using `Type-Led-4` and `B2`; do not silently convert it into a ten-product Still a Find launch. Guest Design Maker previews in [PR #142](https://github.com/kyleliaw-candoit/t-shirt/pull/142) do not prove an authenticated Printful account or production approval.

**Strategy approval of the existing Gate 1 proposal is not granted by this review.** Three points must be resolved before it is interpretable:

1. **Founder active minutes:** recover a defensible comparable WTD Phase A estimate or range, with exclusions and uncertainty. Timestamp gaps, message counts and handoffs are not labor minutes. If a range is accepted, pre-register a conservative comparison against its lower bound rather than selecting a favorable value after Shopify results exist. Keep non-comparable Phase B work separate.
2. **Equivalent elapsed clocks:** the WTD timestamp proxies are gross elapsed, whereas #110 proposes net Shopify delivery with excluded waits. Either compare gross to gross, or reconstruct defensible equivalent exclusions on both sides. Subtracting waiting only from Shopify cannot establish the claimed improvement. Report gross, net and active effort separately.
3. **Exact thresholds:** a 60% reduction from 7h24m means at most **2h57m36s**, not three hours. A 50% reduction from 24h02m means at most **12h01m**; a 12-hour operating target is stricter and compatible. Set exact inequalities before starting. Two products versus ten also limits any whole-store comparison; no normalized ten-product savings can be inferred from those totals alone.

### Original gate or explicit amendment, not a waiver

**Preferred:** preserve the original experiment if a credible Founder-minute baseline and equivalent clocks can be recovered cheaply. Strategy and Kyle then approve the baseline, exact thresholds and clock rules before implementation; the existing quality, privacy, mapping and review gates remain.

**If historical active minutes are genuinely unrecoverable:** recommend a reviewed charter amendment before implementation. It may test prospective absolute burden ceilings, such as no more than 60 active Founder minutes and explicitly chosen elapsed ceilings, while preserving quality and measurement acceptance. It must remove the unsupported claim of proving 50% less historical Founder effort. A three-hour ceiling could be chosen as a new absolute criterion, but must not be described as meeting the original 60% reduction. Define amended disposition language in advance: passing a bounded feasibility test is not retrospectively passing the original comparative-efficiency test or automatically establishing Shopify as the company default.

This is an alternative for explicit approval, not a change enacted by this document. Do not start, measure selectively or award Pass/Conditional pass while the old gate remains unresolved.

### Keep the immediate Founder checkpoint small

Use the answers to the already-pending account/readiness questions rather than asking them again. The remaining Gate 1 factual question is: **Can Kyle supply a defensible range for comparable WTD active minutes, or confirm that it cannot be recovered?** Existing Shopify/Printful account status and a store URL support the access preflight; credentials and identifiers do not belong in GitHub. Then present **one exact Gate 1 contract** for Strategy/Founder approval, original or explicitly amended.

Collect EIN/bank/assumed-name status privately for the broader launch audit, but do not turn those facts, a trademark filing, every future social account or the full launch approval list into prerequisites for deciding the name-independent two-product planning gate.
