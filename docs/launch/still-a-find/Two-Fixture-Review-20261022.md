## 3. Unbranded meaning: Brand Manager decision

**B2: accept for private use.** The front artwork contains the generic ANTIQUES sign, right-pointing arrow and “No, Really. / Five Minutes.” payoff, not a business name, logo or initials. The sign supplies the situation before the conversational promise supplies the humor. It preserves recognition of the habitual detour and affectionate self-deception rather than merely announcing a love of old objects.

**Type-Led-4: accept for private use.** “Thrill / OF THE / Find” remains the main expression, without a leading “The.” The exact replacement “ANTIQUES. VINTAGE. DISCOVERY.” maintains the category anchor while removing the old brand sentence; the compass star is absent. The removal does not leave a semantic hole because the lettering carries excitement and the support line locates that excitement in the intended world. This is an attention/type-led fixture, not a claim of deep humor. At very small presentation sizes the support line is subordinate, so page-level apparel/category context remains necessary.

Both fit the current overlooked-character/discovery positioning. Neither requires putting Still a Find or its line on the artwork. This accepts brand fit, not legal clearance, public release or customer demand.

## 4. Reconstruction and typography: Creative Director decision

**B2: retain unchanged for private use.** The sign remains the first read; the rust arrow is unmistakably right-pointing. The heavier, rounder payoff still feels spoken rather than institutional, with the expected two-line rhythm. Weathering is visibly stronger around the sign edge and through the arrow/post than in the archived source. It reads as sign age, not unrelated decoration, and does not defeat the sign-to-payoff sequence in the saved shirt/provider views. The cream sign field is a deliberate illustrated object, not an accidental opaque rectangle around the entire composition.

Tradeoff: reconstruction is bolder and less restrained in texture than the original. Do not call it an exact extraction or verified font match. No private-use correction is required; any production preparation must preserve ANTIQUES letter counters, arrow direction, grass/post separation, the comma, terminal periods and readable payoff rather than using indiscriminate cleanup.

**Type-Led-4: retain unchanged for private use.** The steep slant, large sweeping Thrill/Find gestures, compact OF THE bridge and rust underline preserve the fashion-first architecture. The support line follows the rising direction without competing with the script. The derivative is not letter-for-letter identical to the archived artwork, but its altered strokes do not introduce a wrong word or broken reading order in the inspected views. There is sufficient optical separation for the words and underline to remain distinct at the selected shirt scale.

Tradeoff: the tiny category line is the first element to weaken as the shirt image shrinks; the selected footprint is preferable to shrinking the whole artwork. Do not enlarge beyond the selected footprint to rescue UI legibility: native detail is only 151.86 PPI there. Use adequate image area and inspect the eventual mobile render instead. No font substitution, new lettering or artwork generation is recommended for this private test.

These are aesthetic judgments, not guarantees of wearability or purchase interest.

## 5. Partial alpha and production boundary

**Creative decision for both: retain existing alpha unchanged for private simulation only. Production release remains on hold.**

The original master-byte checks reproduce the recorded fractions below alpha 240 among **all nonzero-alpha pixels**: B2 approximately 12.19%; Type-Led-4 approximately 36.22%. This denominator includes faint background pixels. The [distribution record](https://github.com/kyleliaw-candoit/t-shirt/blob/760c1e7832b7078f9f55d75178e4ff58868810ef/docs/creative/design-language/applications/MVV-PRINTFUL-01/evidence/alpha-distribution.json) places about 8.02% and 25.34%, respectively, in alpha 1–15. Thus “36% of the printed ink is translucent” would be misleading. Nevertheless, much remaining ink is near-opaque rather than exactly 255; neither a favorable visual composite nor Good / 150 DPI certifies underbase/opacity behavior.

Before production, Creative and Operations should agree a **new version** that removes unintended faint background haze, preserves intentional distress as open holes, and gives intended ink an accepted opacity treatment. Do not flatten onto Ivory, fill all distress, or apply a blanket threshold without comparing the result. The alpha≥16 bounding rule is a measurement convention, not approved cleanup.

Then re-review exact new master/layout bytes, small lettering, B2 sign/arrow/payoff and Type-Led-4 script/support line on the selected footprint; verify the new shortlisted file in the provider and inspect an authorized physical sample for detail, color/opacity, placement and garment behavior. No sample order or cleanup is authorized by this review.
