**Three mandatory numeric criteria.** Founder active effort from START through the recorded Gate 9 disposition must be **≤3,600 seconds**, including independent Technical Review responses, remediation, re-review and Founder decision/recording effort. START to first reviewable must be **≤10,800 gross elapsed seconds**. START to accepted candidate must be **≤43,200 gross elapsed seconds**. Extending the Founder-effort endpoint beyond #110's pre-Technical-Review accepted candidate is an explicitly proposed change, not an interpretation of the original rule.

**START.** Technical Lead records UTC immediately before the first in-scope Shopify/Printful/store implementation mutation, after Gates 1–3 are satisfied: approved contract, complete reviewed Store Build Packet, exact approved costs, required access and safeguards. Confirm review-owner availability before starting, without scheduling anything through this proposal. The 60-minute cap measures post-START execution effort, not total setup burden. Report pre-START Founder effort separately, including contract review, account/access preflight and packet decisions, and report total Founder effort across preparation and execution. **First reviewable.** One exact private URL/theme version simultaneously has password protection, an unpublished working theme, both realistically configured products, approved images/copy/prices/variants and canonical `design_id`, representative Printful mapping recorded, minimum measurement installed, desktop and compact-mobile renders, and no known blocker to Creative/Brand review. A draft PR or admin record does not qualify.

**Accepted candidate.** Retain #110’s milestone: Creative and Brand accept the exact rendered candidate, ordinary corrections are complete, mismatch proof passes, and the candidate is ready for independent Technical Review. This is not final Gate 9 acceptance. This milestone stops only the accepted-candidate elapsed clock. Founder effort continues accumulating against the same 3,600-second cap through Gate 9. Record subsequent technical-review corrections, operator effort and gross time to final disposition; no final Pass or Conditional pass exists before those reviews and the complete Founder-effort total are recorded.

**Clocks and holds.** Gross equals milestone UTC minus START and never pauses. Net equals gross minus the union of documented intervals wholly blocking progress while awaiting a complete Founder/specialist handoff response, verified external outage recovery or access approval outside operator control. Log hold start/end, cause, owner and evidence; missing records and overlapping productive work cannot be excluded. Count overlapping holds once. Include implementation, retries, rendering, ordinary propagation, connector limitations, rework and recovery. Log Founder active sessions separately from passive waiting, plus operator effort by stage and first-/second-product timing.

Gross is the decision clock because it captures actual delivery availability and prevents selective exclusions; net diagnoses the cause. Safety or approval holds always stop prohibited work even while gross continues. Never accelerate through a permission gate. An unfinished run is **Blocked/inconclusive**, not Pass; a completed run exceeding any ceiling is **Fail against this contract**, with causes recorded. Never silently restart clocks. Any invalid measurement requires a documented, justified, pre-interpretation Strategy/Founder decision under the charter; retain the original record.

**Disposition.** Pass requires all three ceilings and every unchanged scope, experience, creative-control, operational, mapping, measurement, privacy and independent-review gate. Conditional pass requires those same numeric and safety/quality gates plus a narrow, demonstrated repeatable workaround with ongoing effort/cost recorded; it cannot waive a missed ceiling or missing proof. Neither outcome establishes historical savings or automatically makes Shopify the default. Strategy recommends architecture disposition; Founder separately decides adoption.
