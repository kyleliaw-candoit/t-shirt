# Coverage and limitations

[Audit](README.md) · [Material bank](Material-Bank.md) · [Handoff](Creative-Director-Handoff.md)

The bank contains **19 entries: 13 participant reports and 6 technical/convention entries**, based on **15 source pages on 14 domains** (counting the Reddit thread and its comment permalinks as one page). Eight entries explicitly include flea markets or swap meets: **E001, E002, E003, E004, E005, E008, E009, E010**. These are coverage counts, not independent-source or frequency counts; E009/E010 share a source, and E005 includes family history.

## Evidence gaps addressed

| Gap | Strongest contribution | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Episodes and language | Specific actions, decisions, interactions and speaker-linked expressions | Memorable public stories are selected; expressions are not automatically common slang |
| Technical knowledge | Six entries matched to museum, conservation or platform authority | Broad recognition unmeasured; silver, Pyrex and vinyl literacy differ |
| Emotional contradictions | Explicit feelings plus restraint/helpful-partner counterexamples | Regret oversampled; professional conservation disagreement does not prove lay hostility |

The coverage imbalance is **materially reduced, not eliminated**. Flea/swap markets supply actual episodes rather than just audience labels. Everyday vintage objects have substance, though kitchenware and collecting specialists remain prominent. Distinct material areas now include personal history, hunting routines, knowledge with uncertainty, acquisition/use constraints, seller relationships and object care. These are evidence areas, not concepts or recipe rankings.

## Limits that must travel with the bank

1. English-language, purposive qualitative evidence; no prevalence, demographics, audience size, apparel demand or purchase-intent inference.
2. Accounts span 2008–2025 where dates are known; not current price evidence or proof of unchanged norms.
3. Participant attributions, values and chronology remain reports unless separately verified. Never turn an uncertain signature into a famous-maker reveal.
4. Source dependence: E007/E008; E009/E010; E011/E012/E019. E004 uses a main post and comments on one page. Do not count these as independent confirmations.
5. E012's object age is unknown; E009/E010 include mixed-age collectibles. They support broader finding behaviors, not a claim that all secondhand objects are vintage.
6. Technical facts do not establish audience familiarity. Runouts and hallmarks require narrower expertise than ordinary shopping logistics.
7. Universal “one more booth,” antagonistic spouses, compulsive buying and broad contempt for refinishing remain unestablished here.
8. No participant contact, external posting, purchases or paid access occurred.

## Bounded future work

If a selected brief depends on it, add recent dedicated vintage-shop/mall episodes, a firsthand preservation/alteration exchange, or casual noncollector experience in another object category. These gaps do not require restarting market validation or TRACE decoding. The current bank supports a varied evidence handoff with its limits attached.
