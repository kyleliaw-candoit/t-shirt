## Evidence-safe use

Carry the stable ID, source, segment, setting, recognition prerequisite and limitation into any later brief. Keep three questions separate:

1. Is the underlying fact or reported experience supported?
2. Does the intended audience know enough to recognize it without explanation?
3. Does the eventual creative expression produce a useful response?

This work addresses the first and supplies bounded hypotheses for the second. It does not answer the third. Verified facts may be obscure; vivid anecdotes may be unrepresentative. E001 reports attachment/generosity; E003 restraint; E008 delight. E014–E018 do not establish participant feelings simply because their facts suggest a tension.

## Do not promote to established truth

- All hunters recognize makers, patterns or runout codes.
- Finds must be rare, expensive, antique or profitable.
- All hunters are resellers, rescuers, nostalgic or compulsive.
- Partners disapprove; some accounts here show practical help/shared hunting.
- Patina means never clean: active deterioration is different.
- A mark alone proves age, maker, provenance or value.
- Merchandise listings, engagement or memorable stories prove apparel demand.
