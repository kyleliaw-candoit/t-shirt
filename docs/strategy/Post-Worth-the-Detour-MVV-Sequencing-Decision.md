# Post-Worth the Detour MVV Sequencing Decision

## Decision

After the current **Worth the Detour / R012 Antiques Treasure Hunters** MVV, the recommended sequence is:

1. **MVV #2 — R010 Remote Working Parents**
2. **MVV #3 — R028 Home Baristas / Home Espresso Hobbyists**
3. **Then compare market learning across the three tests before commissioning a broad new research wave or locking the next opportunity.**

R002 Vietnamese Coffee Culture remains a high-priority reserve candidate and should return to the front of the queue if the Home Espresso test supports the broader **Specificity / Ritual / Independent Taste** hypothesis.

## Why R010 Remote Working Parents stays second

R010 should follow Worth the Detour because it tests a materially different identity mechanism rather than another enthusiast/hobby identity.

Worth the Detour primarily tests:
- discovery / discernment;
- identity built around noticing hidden value;
- a lifestyle/worldview brand hypothesis;
- a practiced interest with visible behavioral rituals.

Remote Working Parents tests:
- role collision / life condition;
- strong “that is exactly me” recognition without requiring hobby jargon;
- whether identity resonance can support apparel when direct merchandise-category proof is less mature;
- whether the company can successfully serve identities outside classic hobby and enthusiast communities.

The purpose is portfolio learning, not merely selecting the identity with the highest standalone score.

If R010 performs well, the company gains evidence that role-collision and life-condition identities are a viable opportunity-generation class. If it performs poorly under a fair test, that is also valuable evidence about the limits of recognition without stronger public-signaling or merchandise behavior.

## Why R028 Home Espresso is third

R028 is currently the preferred third test ahead of R002 Vietnamese Coffee because it provides a clean test of a strategically important hypothesis:

> **Can the company find and serve a narrow practiced identity hidden inside a huge generic consumption category?**

The relevant distinction is not “coffee lover.” It is the specific identity of someone who dials in espresso at home and cares about workflow, dose/yield, grinders, puck preparation, extraction, milk texture, equipment care, and repeatability.

R028 combines:
- strong identity resonance;
- visible market proof;
- comparatively promising differentiated territory inside a crowded broader category;
- very high creative depth;
- excellent MVP testability;
- high participation/apparel fit;
- sufficient Founder Advantage to reduce some execution risk.

It therefore provides a strong test of **practiced specificity inside a broad market**.

## Why R002 Vietnamese Coffee remains important but moves behind R028

R002 remains commercially and strategically attractive, especially because it may have real affinity with R028 Home Espresso and, more cautiously, R001 Matcha.

However, Vietnamese Coffee carries additional cultural-positioning responsibility. It should not be flattened into “another coffee method” or generalized beverage-enthusiast language.

Home Espresso is therefore the cleaner first test of the broader **Specificity / Ritual / Independent Taste** mechanism. If that mechanism performs, R002 becomes more strategically valuable because the next question becomes whether the worldview extends into culturally specific beverage and food identities while preserving their distinct context.

## Why not launch a third broad research wave yet

Do **not** immediately commission R041–R060 as another broad twenty-identity batch.

The company now has forty researched identities. The current bottleneck is not idea supply; it is market evidence.

The more valuable question is now:

> **Which kinds of identity actually produce customer behavior when presented through real offers and credible brand contexts?**

Additional broad research before accumulating several differentiated market tests risks increasing research sophistication without resolving the more important commercial uncertainty.

## Preferred use of future research

Future research should become more **targeted and decision-driven**.

Examples:

If **Worth the Detour** shows traction, commission a small adjacency batch around the brand world, such as:
- preservation-minded old-house people;
- architectural salvage hunters;
- estate-sale regulars;
- sea-glass hunters / beachcombers where relevant;
- rare / used bookstore hunters;
- record diggers;
- other discovery/stewardship adjacencies that are both lifestyle- and brand-plausible.

If **Home Espresso** shows traction, commission a small adjacency batch around Specificity / Ritual / Independent Taste, such as:
- pour-over / manual-brew people;
- home coffee roasters;
- specialty café / independent roastery regulars;
- other narrow ritual identities where broad-category consumers become practiced insiders.

The research question should then be:

> **“We have evidence this worldview may work. Where does its credible brand neighborhood end?”**

—not merely “What else can we research?”

## Three-test learning portfolio

The recommended first three MVVs intentionally test different opportunity-generation mechanisms:

| MVV | Identity | Primary learning hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| #1 | R012 Antiques Treasure Hunters / Worth the Detour | Discovery / worldview identity with a coherent brand neighborhood |
| #2 | R010 Remote Working Parents | Role collision / life-condition identity with weaker mature merch proof but strong recognition |
| #3 | R028 Home Espresso Hobbyists | Practiced specificity / enthusiast identity hidden inside a broad generic category |

After these three tests, compare not only raw traffic or design performance but what each test teaches about:
- identity-to-purchase conversion;
- brand-context strength;
- differentiated acquisition potential;
- downstream product interest;
- adjacent browsing / brand-world coherence;
- repeatable opportunity-generation mechanisms;
- whether secondary T-shirt value layers strengthened or merely accompanied identity demand.

## Decision Rule After MVV #3

Do not pre-commit today to MVV #4.

After Worth the Detour, Remote Working Parents, and Home Espresso have produced comparable market evidence, decide whether the next unit of effort should be:

1. **R002 Vietnamese Coffee Culture**;
2. another existing R001–R040 candidate;
3. a small targeted adjacency research batch around a validated worldview/brand cluster; or
4. a broader new identity research wave only if the existing opportunity inventory is no longer sufficient.

## Governing principle

> **Use the early portfolio to learn what kinds of identities this company is structurally good at finding and serving—not merely which individual niche produces the prettiest pre-launch score.**

This sequencing preserves learning velocity, portfolio diversity, and earned complexity while keeping the market—not research confidence—as the final arbiter.
