# Pass 1 Calibration — R021–R040

## Purpose

This calibration compares the twenty independently assessed R021–R040 identities before broader R001–R040 cross-identity synthesis.

The goal is **not** to rank mechanically by summed score. The six dimensions are comparison aids. Commercial structure, identity-to-purchase logic, differentiation, founder execution risk, T-shirt value-stack behavior, testability, and information value remain more important than arithmetic.

## Calibration Result

The four batch assessments are sufficiently consistent to compare directly. No numerical rating changes are required after cross-batch review.

The central R021–R040 finding is:

> **Strong identity is abundant. Strong identity plus a credible independent T-shirt purchase bridge plus entrant whitespace is much rarer.**

The Value Stack lens also produced a useful refinement:

> **A T-shirt can naturally belong to an activity and still be a weak entrant opportunity if the market is crowded or if a stronger native identity object already dominates signaling.**

Examples include Woodworking, Knitting/Crochet, and Cruise People.

# Calibration Tiers

## Tier A — Cleanest commercial test candidates

### R025 — Geocachers

The strongest combination of behavioral specificity, direct identity signaling, creative depth, relative whitespace, and MVP fit in the new wave. Founder Advantage is weak, so insider validation is a required execution control rather than a reason to abandon the opportunity.

### R027 — Preservation-Minded Old-House People

Smaller direct apparel proof than many categories, but unusually strong worldview identity and comparatively open territory. The stewardship / repair / trained-appreciation mechanism is strategically distinct from generic renovation.

### R028 — Home Baristas / Home Espresso Hobbyists

A narrow practiced identity with strong ritual frequency, natural T-shirt fit, good creative depth, and a plausible wedge inside the much larger crowded coffee universe. Founder familiarity is modest but materially better than most new-wave maker/specialist identities.

### R035 — Weather Enthusiasts

The least market-validated Tier A candidate, but potentially the cleanest fragmented-niche experiment. Strong recurring behavior and insider recognition coexist with relatively small direct apparel supply. This is exactly the kind of uncertainty a cheap market test can resolve.

## Tier A2 — High-value experiments, but with a structural condition

These deserve serious consideration in the later R001–R040 portfolio synthesis because they test strategically important hypotheses, even if they are not as clean as Tier A.

### R022 — Mahjong Players

Very strong identity, social visibility, and participation utility. **Mandatory condition:** choose a specific rules/cultural variant before creative development. The umbrella is not a valid single execution voice.

### R026 — Disc Golfers

One of the best tests of the Participation Utility hypothesis because the shirt is directly worn during peer-facing participation. The challenge is whether behavior-native creative can break through an already competitive apparel ecosystem.

### R030 — Garment Sewists / Home Garment Makers

A uniquely informative test of the **native identity-object substitution** problem. Identity and clothing relevance are extremely high, yet customers may prefer to make the identity-bearing garment themselves. A market test can answer something research cannot.

## Tier B — Attractive, but wedge burden is substantial

- **R021 Potters / Ceramic Artists** — excellent identity and participation utility; obvious merchandise heavily crowded.
- **R024 Film Photographers** — strong process/worldview identity and aesthetic value; generic analog-camera territory mature.
- **R029 Mushroom Foragers** — excellent discovery identity; generic mushroom/cottagecore aesthetics muddy the market.
- **R032 Railfans** — exceptional identity and demand, but strongest signals are often specific protected affiliations.
- **R033 Chiliheads** — strong value stack and behavior; founder fluency low and generic spicy-food territory mature.
- **R037 Genealogists / Family-History Researchers** — deep emotional/process identity; mature gifting market and unusually sensitive subject matter.

These remain viable reserve candidates. They should not be tested generically.

## Tier C — Watch / later

- **R023 Woodworkers** — high demand and very high participation utility, but crowded supply plus 1/10 Founder Advantage leaves little entrant advantage.
- **R031 Knitters & Crocheters** — huge identity and mature market; handmade garments often provide a stronger native signal than printed tees.
- **R034 Amateur Astronomers** — rich identity, but generic space/celestial competition is enormous and founder fluency is minimal.
- **R036 Fountain Pen & Stationery Enthusiasts** — rich identity with whitespace, but the apparel purchase bridge is less proven and higher-utility objects dominate spending.
- **R038 Repeat Cruisers** — extremely strong purchase behavior and participation utility, but one of the most commoditized personalized-shirt markets in the wave.
- **R039 Farmers-Market Regulars** — strongest Founder Advantage in R021–R040, but weaker universal self-labeling and stronger utility in market-specific totes/carrying objects.
- **R040 Romance Readers** — extraordinary identity and demand, but severe saturation, subgenre fragmentation, trend speed, IP constraints, and 1/10 Founder Advantage.

# Cross-Batch Calibration Findings

## 1. Competitive Opportunity remains the strongest separator

R021–R040 reinforces Round 1: identity strength, creative depth, and demand validation are common. A credible place for a new entrant to stand is scarce.

R038 Cruise People and R040 Romance Readers are the clearest examples. Both have extraordinary purchase evidence; neither is currently a clean entrant opportunity.

## 2. Participation Utility is a multiplier, not a selection rule

The data now shows at least three different outcomes:

- **High utility + cleaner opportunity:** R028 Home Espresso, to a degree R026 Disc Golf.
- **High utility + crowded opportunity:** R021 Pottery, R023 Woodworking, R038 Cruise People.
- **Lower utility + cleaner opportunity:** R035 Weather Enthusiasts and R027 Old-House People in many contexts.

Therefore, the founder hypothesis is directionally useful but should not become a dominant score.

## 3. Add a new qualitative question: what is the native identity object?

Several identities have powerful non-T-shirt signals:

- sewists → self-made clothing;
- knitters/crocheters → handmade fiber garments;
- fountain-pen people → pens, ink, notebooks;
- railfans → railroad/route-specific affiliation objects;
- film photographers → cameras and photographs;
- amateur astronomers → telescopes/images/program credentials.

A T-shirt opportunity is stronger when it **complements** these signals rather than competes with them.

This is a strategic observation, not yet a new scored dimension.

## 4. Founder Advantage changes execution risk, not identity truth

R025 Geocachers and R035 Weather Enthusiasts remain serious candidates despite low Founder Advantage because their structural opportunity is attractive. The correct response is stronger insider validation.

Conversely, R039 Farmers-Market Regulars does not become a Tier A opportunity merely because founder familiarity is high. Founder Advantage cannot substitute for a weaker identity-to-purchase gate.

## 5. Narrow practiced identities often outperform broad lifestyle umbrellas

R028 Home Espresso is more attractive than generic coffee. R035 Weather Enthusiasts is more interesting than generic “science/weather” merchandising. R036 only becomes strategically coherent when narrowed toward fountain-pen users rather than broad stationery.

The company should continue looking for **specific practiced identities inside broad validated categories**.

## 6. Some of the best experiments are valuable because they can fail informatively

R030 Garment Sewists is a good example. A weak result would teach whether identity resonance converts when the audience already manufactures its own strongest identity apparel. That is useful portfolio learning even if the market itself does not advance.

# Calibration Decision

R021–R040 calibration is complete.

The **clean commercial candidate set** entering the broader R001–R040 synthesis is:

> **R025, R027, R028, R035**

The **high-information conditional candidate set** is:

> **R022, R026, R030**

Tier B candidates remain reserves rather than being discarded.

The next Strategy step is not a standalone R021–R040 final ranking. It is the founder-requested **R001–R040 cross-identity / cluster synthesis**, where all forty researched identities are considered together and unresearched adjacent identities may be surfaced as future research candidates.
