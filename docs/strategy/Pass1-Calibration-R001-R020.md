# Pass 1 Calibration — R001–R020

## Purpose

State 3 calibrates the independently produced Pass 1 assessments across all twenty researched identities before cross-identity synthesis.

The goal is not to mechanically rank by summed score. The six ratings are comparison aids; the commercial thesis, competition structure, founder execution advantage, MVP testability, and strategic disposition remain more important than arithmetic.

## Calibration Result

The independently produced batches are sufficiently consistent to compare directly. No numeric rating changes are required after cross-batch review.

The most important portfolio-level calibration is structural:

> **High demand is common in the portfolio. Credible whitespace plus executable learning is scarce.**

Several identities have stronger raw demand than the leading test candidates but weaker entrant-specific opportunity because the obvious category is heavily commoditized or incumbent-dominated.

## Calibration Tiers

### Tier A — Strong near-term test candidates

These identities combine a credible commercial wedge with good execution and testability.

- **R012 Antiques Treasure Hunters** — strongest balance of validated demand, behavioral depth, Founder Advantage, competitive openness, and MVP fit.
- **R019 No-Hustle Hikers** — strong counter-position, strong Founder Advantage, excellent MVP fit, and differentiated territory beyond the crowded slow-animal motif.
- **R010 Remote Working Parents** — comparatively open market, strong lived-condition recognition, strong Founder Advantage, and unusually high information value.
- **R002 Vietnamese Coffee Culture** — credible whitespace and the highest Founder Advantage among serious candidates, offset by narrower direct apparel validation and cultural-positioning sensitivity.
- **R017 Food-Travel People** — open intersection identity and excellent testability, offset by weaker explicit identity-apparel proof and weaker universal self-labeling.

### Tier B — Attractive but requires a sharper wedge

These identities are commercially credible but should not enter through generic category positioning.

R001 Matcha; R003 National Parks; R004 Mechanical Keyboards; R006 Sourdough; R008 Grandparents; R009 Roller Skaters; R011 Beach People; R014 Gardeners; R015 Birders; R016 Climbers / Boulderers; R018 Puzzle People; R020 Houseplant People.

Common reason: direct demand is visible, but obvious territory is crowded or the strongest remaining opportunity requires deeper insider fluency than the company currently possesses.

### Tier C — Watch / later

- **R007 Pickleball Players** — huge validated market, but squeezed between commodity novelty merch and increasingly sophisticated dedicated sport brands; very low Founder Advantage.
- **R013 Runners** — exceptional identity and demand, but one of the most professionally served categories in the portfolio.

### Tier D — Deprioritize

- **R005 Swifties / Taylor Swift Fandom** — enormous demand does not compensate for incumbent dominance, saturation, low Founder Advantage, and dependence on creator-controlled cultural material.

## Cross-Batch Calibration Findings

### 1. Competitive Opportunity is the strongest separator

Many identities score 4–5 on identity-to-purchase, market evidence, and creative depth. The scarce asset is a credible place to stand.

### 2. Founder Advantage matters most when differentiation requires insider depth

Low Founder Advantage is especially costly in R004, R006, R015, R016, and R018 because their remaining whitespace lies below the generic surface layer.

### 3. Weak direct merch evidence can increase test value when the identity is otherwise strong

R010, R017, and R019 are not penalized into irrelevance by less mature direct apparel categories. Their uncertainty is exactly what a cheap market test can resolve.

### 4. The best first-wave candidates are not the biggest categories

R012, R019, and R010 are strategically cleaner than many larger categories because they combine credible differentiation, Founder Advantage, and efficient learning.

## State 3 Decision

State 3 is complete.

The calibrated serious-candidate set entering Pass 2 is:

Tier B identities remain available as reserves and future portfolio replacements rather than being discarded.
